The Death-eater's powers are not infinite. If you look at a creature's capacity to be resurrected, you can convert that to potential energy and understand how ludicrous of a power source keeping things from being fully dead is. While creatures are technically alive under the full influence of Car-teruna, they are barely so, as it eats away all of the potential energy that could have revived them.
