====== Pot Luck ======
Created Tuesday 02 January 2024

Life is hard. Life is harder still when you're down on your luck, or if you never started with any luck at all, but sometimes the only way to keep rice in your bowl is to steal it from someone else's mouth. In three weeks time, you and your //associates// are attending a dinner you haven't been invited to. The host doesn't know you're coming and hopefully won't know you're there or leaving. The reason you're going isn't the food though that could be a sweet bonus. You're heading in to grab an extremely valuable teapot from the home of Kei Barigai. The teapot, aside from producing an unlimited amount of tea, it occasionaly grants wishes. Even if it isn't going to grant a wish, and it might, it's worth a hefty payday. Anyone who manages to sell that pot is set for life, at least living in Keina, maybe even Gossogi. Even split, it's more than enough to change some luck

Character creation rules: Rogues and Bards only


**The Actors**
	The party - a Gallery of Rogues
	Kei Barigai - A distracted host
	Athinacaraman - a one-eyed ancient dragon disguised to whom the law is absolute
	Masta - a butler who despises parties for their disorder
