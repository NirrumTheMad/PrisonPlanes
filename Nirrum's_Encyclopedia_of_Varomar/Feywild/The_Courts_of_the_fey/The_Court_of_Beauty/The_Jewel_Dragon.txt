====== The Jewel Dragon ======
Created Tuesday 19 November 2024

A draconic children's story, translated from A southern Draconic Dialect. The original is made of rhyming couplets without overarching meter, both the first and last syllables are intended to rhyme. 
Translator's notes: 
* The word Gather is inaccurate, hoard is inaccurate, collect is inaccurate. The word dragons use for it is cognate with their word for *rule*. It implies it is both a part of them, something they control, and something they were intended to have.
* Lands Might be better translated as Feif or Herd. Ranch?
* The Draconic term for a show of supplication is to lower one's wings, placing them on the ground.



Every drake, every serpent, every wyrm knows that it is the right of all dragons to rule. We show our rule in many ways and it is our way to show our rule as is our right. 
Many dragons gather Bones, Many Dragons gather Towns, Many Dragons gather great <lands> of meat.  Most Dragons keep stones, Gems, and Metals like gold and Iron.
One Dragon is scaled in treasure, their tongue, eyes, claws and great feathered wings. They are the Wendhariramonknikhuai, and their treasure comes with them.

The Wendhariramonknikhuai once flew out from their Fane in distant lands, and fought the master of the shining hall.
The Master of the shining hall had bent its knee to unscaled things, and its hoard was  Mostly gone. 
The Wendhariramonknikhuai told the stony lord. "I will take your halls, for all things that shine are mine"

But the deposed master posed a challenge to the shining dragon, "Find those who bested me, Force them to Lower their wings to you"
"Then I will grant thee my halls and my name" Said the Master, who knew these unscaled ones to be very strong.
