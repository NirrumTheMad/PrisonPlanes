====== Adventure Formula ======
Created Sunday 07 July 2024

I use this formula for Travelling adventures. This won't work with complex puzzles per se, but it's excellent for overland journeys, or underland journeys as I first used it. 

Actors:
	A list of all major (and some minor) players and groups, each followed by their relationship to the situation
	**The bad guys:**
		Member, Description
	**The Party:**
		Member, description


**The setting:**
	A brief description of where this takes place. Settings only describe the environment, not the path, though sometimes environments change.

===== The First Part =====
Here, a description of how this *should* feel, how it is described to the players.

	**Path: **a description of the actual routes available. High, low, Secrets, irritations, blessings. Include the map here if one is available.
	**Win condition:** When does this part of the adventure end? How do they move to the next section? *Open the black door* is a good example. List multiple if there are multiple
	**Treasure:** Natural treasures, forgotten treasures, treasures lost or hidden, Treasuress to be discovered, and their context within the path
	**Catches: **What hinders the party in general? Is it warm? are they running low on water? what adversity is known to them?
	**Twists: **What dangers are there that the party does //not// know about, that recontextualises their journey? Not Just normal enemies, but problems, like sudden storms, or someone hunting them. When does this twist show itself?
	**Enemies:** What threats are on the trail, perhaps known about, expected, or neither, but reasonable to be part of the path? What is their context on the path?
