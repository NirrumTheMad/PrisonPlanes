====== ShLu-guum-tk-tktktk ======
Created Sunday 18 August 2024

A home of shelf-slope Sea elves. Composed of a series of shell-like bubbles, Topped with an airlocked water resevoir for fresh water, each built into eachother, the architectural hazards avoided by the buoyancy provided by the water. 

Urban
Shlu-guum-tk-tktktk is a wide and flat snowflake embedded into the sea floor on one of the gentler slopes. Cool water rushes up from lower down the shelf here, pushing sediment against the lower homes, but cooling the whole town, which sits in a warm current. Some weeks, this becomes extremely warm and most people simply rest for days down in dusty lower buildings.
