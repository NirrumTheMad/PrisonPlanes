====== Oath Of Courage ======
Created Monday 19 August 2024

**Courage. **Stand against fear, and that which causes fear
**Compassion. **Know that your actions affect others, Stand as a bulwark from fear, not a cause of it.
**Prudence. **Courage is not stupidity. It is Courageous to admit when you cannot win, and sometimes courageous to try anyway
**Tenacity. **Do not stop because things are difficult.
