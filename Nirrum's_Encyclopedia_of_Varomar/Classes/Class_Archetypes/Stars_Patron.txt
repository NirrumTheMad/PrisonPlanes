====== Stars Patron ======
Created Wednesday 07 February 2024

The Dwarves of Skalmirthon have become the shepherds of stars, which have compressed their power  so they can fit in this world, but that power needs a vent, a way to escape. Those chosen by the stars can turn this power into magic of many kinds. The stars want many things, and offer bizzare insights into magic itself, meaning that pacts with them are easy to obtain.
