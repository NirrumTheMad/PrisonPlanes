**Call to Arms(3)**
	Your allies that can percieve you get a bonus to initiative equal to your charisma modifier
	
**Swiftsinging(3)**
	 A creature that has a Bardic Inspiration die from you can roll that die and gains additional number of feet in movement equal to five times the result until the start of its next turn. 
	
**As one (6)**
	A creature that has a Bardic Inspiration die can use its reaction to move that inspiration to a new creature. 
**Hands of Comrades (6)**
	A creature that has a bardic inspiration die may expend it as a reaction to allow another creature to use their bonus for an ability check
	
**By Blood, Bound (14)**
	Creatures within 20ft of you or a creature with one of your bardic inspirations may spend their own hit die instead of expending the use of bardic inspiration.
