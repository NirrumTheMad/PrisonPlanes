			Bright and deep blue skies, A horizon which seems to change with each step but isn't as flat. glimmering false gold sand, Seawheat and small waves, puffs of occasional steam
			Smells of  Salt, Seaweed and rank fish, with occasional hints of rose
			Sounds of the Fizzaps of laserclams firing, the pops of exploding shrimp, the barks of barracudas, and the low grunts of Lungfish among tiny crickling waves
			The feel of soft grit giving way underfoot, warm waters that are just slightly cooler than the air, The Baking light of the sun, the slime of stepped-on-fish and plants
			The taste of delicate rosy flounder, gamey Lungfish, metallic clam, and salty wheat
			
			
		Wicklands. A smoggy, smokey land of irritants
			The Dim pervasive orange of a smokey sky, the twisted, ignited trees with sparse branches. occasional buildings built of huge tapers and votives. The huge, tangled Novabushes that are taller than trees, but with thin branches and massive clusters of flowers whose petals start blue and transition from green to violet. 
			The perpetual odor of candlesmoke, honey, and fire, only broken by the the frigid scent of the bushes 
			The Prickling sound of igniting branches is married to the hiss of the fire themselves, a droning sussur. Hives of bees harmonise in major organ chords of warning
			The clammy clawing feeling of wax on skin, in lungs, on eyes and hair, the stifling cough that is necessary to continue breathing. One shouldn't linger in this place without the ability to cure diseases
			Everything tastes of wax, except the honey of the shaggy bees which is sweet, slightly savoury, with hints of rosemary

==== A Shallow ocean, Wicklands, Lueri unshinihi ====

	The path: Any direction will lead appropriately
	
	
		
	The party awakens, lying flat on their back, staring up at a warm summer sky. At least one is dry on a sandbar, and the rest in a not-very-deep sheet of water, Seawheat tickling their face, tiny shrimp trying to feed on the dead skin that's exposed, shrimp that explode if disturbed too quickly.
	The Party must learn to spot beds of laserclams and warrior mussels, which function as small landmines while avoiding Sumigi lungfishes. A laughing goat-headed Terror fey asks them if they've brought enough water as it walks along on stilts.  It takes 20 hours of relative time to cross the wicklands. Water is available from the dewy flounders
	
	The Flat ocean Edges on wavy wax which covers over the water like a sheet, a thin sheet that breaks for the first few hundred steps until eventually the lightest party members can walk unimpeded on the wax. Heavier and heavily armored characters go last. With each step, the sun seems to go sideways. It doesn't set as one would normally expect, there always seems to be a bright yellow-blue line that fades from day to night until the party is in the wicklands proper. if they walk back at all, the day rotates back to meet them
	
	Twists: The Sumigi are extremely opportune but relatively weak
	
	The wicklands are a forest of glowing orange, with thick, almost toxic air in which constitution saving throws need to be made to cough up wax that builds up in the lungs twice during their time. Exhaustion points come from failed saves. The trees are ropey and there are small fires around their bases and at the tips of their branches. Carver gnomes work to scoop up the wax. 
	
	huge Jaws loom out from behind a tree and if anyone reacts, a terror fey giggles as it collects their fright in a bag and dips into a pool of wax, disappearing
	A single braatus Jumps the party, screaming Monotonosly
	
	A hazy town built of tall votive candles provides respite from the burning pits of wax. Though there is little worth buying and little that can be traded, save rations, 
	The town is Jalahzo Gon, and the townsfolk are delighted to tell people that it is going to be the location of a fireheart tomorrow

	
	Here, the party is assaulted also by basar salter-yin, Fierce infernal Soul Tearer, Friedrich Fentiman, and Mohipinrolo furi-molada, Preparing a ritual to let the godking know when the fireheart erupts.
	Mohipinrolo has of course made friends all over town and is having a great time. But Fierce, Friedrich, and Basar are miserable and are they are the ones who start the fight. 
	Mohipinrolo can't let the party go if they fight his brothers, He liked his brothers, even if they were murderous bastards, and he can't finish the ritual without their help, and he can't just stop, his father would kill him. 
	
	However the party resolves this, they are pulled toward the nearest Castle of the Fey Courts, Lueri unshinihi. For an appropriate amount of favour they learn how to navigate Unshinihi's Grounds,
		
		"You seek the power of the Principal of curses. The Master of True ghosts, Perhaps?"
		"To get to lueri unshinihi, take that path to the edge of sundown, where the sky is still golden and lit. There you will find a manor of simple make, Wood logs stacked high, wide windows that feel small. It is cozy, as that is the province of the court of terror as much as that of glamour. Walk up the honeysuckle path until winter."
		"Should you leave the path, you will be lost. Though some less malevolent lords will let you return. after the 13th arch, you may find the Mizzuraer, of reaping and collection, or after the 30th arch, you may speak with the logoch, Of fading senses. At the 49th, the Creyhundeeth, of imminent disaster. The Blezzangionan, who you seek is a guest of the Lord of Terror, within the cabin itself.
	
	
	The journey is short, Lueri unshinihi is not far away from Jalahzo Gon, though it seems bordered by a relatively simple pine forest.
	
	
	Its pathway is paved in blue shale among overgrown green grass. The eyes of Fey eels look out through the grass
			The Creyhundeeth offers:
				**A goodberry bush, which needs daily watering and casts the spell goodberry once a day. **It is resistant to frost and snow but does poorly in heat. It prefers partial sunlight and very well drained soil, which must be partially wood fibres. 
				**An Acorn. **Shortly after leaving this area, a tiny baby fey emerges, proclaims itself Shewilladima, and tells the party to call her The Modwila at all times so she can keep her name, and shows the party immense love. 
					Shewilladima knows her favour belongs to the party, but she does not have much. she can grant a total of 19,341 favour, after which time, she will perish.
					Shewilladima is a member of the court of Valour. She is courageous but not suicidal. She has other ambitions, such as becoming big enough to hold a sword or even use a bow.
					She will sacrifice herself wether the party wants her to or not if they are going to lose something they love when they exit the feywild.
					She will help the party as best she can but she is CR 0. She will hide if there is danger and try to protect things important to the party if she can. She has a lift strength of 10lb
					She is easily scared by stories of violence, but is courageous in the face of it. 
					She can cast two spells: witch bolt once a day and Lightning lure as a cantrip. She has 4hp and a fly speed of 25ft. Her AC is 11
				**A tiny vampiric Mimic that disguises as a compass and tries to bite non-fey that it gets close to. It cannot get through thick leather and needs blood once every two days. More and it will grow, less and it will die.**

	Within Lueri Unshinihi's comfy front hallway, the party is instructed to take off their boots by a rather cheery looking clown puppet who is nailed to the shelf. Underneath the shelf is the line "do not free the clown, Please." If freed, the clown scuttles upstairs and is never seen again.
	
	There is enough room for maybe two or three creatures comfortably and taking off their boots requires an action. 



	Win Condition: Survive the Journey to Lueri unshinihi and bring back a curse for one chosen target to weaken them
