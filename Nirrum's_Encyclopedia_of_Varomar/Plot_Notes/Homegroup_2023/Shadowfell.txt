====== Shadowfell ======
Created Thursday 29 August 2024

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EvYK0MbY3oA

The shadowfell is a series of vignettes That revolve around time travel


Each character gets lost in a memory, a normal day. If they remember to eat, sleep, and drink, then they come back to the shadowfell rested, watered, fed. 

Each character must tend to a garden, until it is overcome by pests. Each time they kill a pest, they are affected by *hurl through hell*. 

Each character partakes in a chase encounter, as a fierce god tries to scrape them from a city with a spindly tree. 

Each character finds themselves unnaturally aged, and must fight a demon without being noticed by people that are trying to take care of them.
