====== Spoiler Ideas ======
Created Saturday 05 October 2024

The blood around the dagger was nearly dried by the time you got the last bit of broken tooth out. It was hard to regrow those. Harder to repair stabbed hearts. Your Father's shirt now sticking to the blade, the smell of what remained of his last meal beginning to fill the house. Good to see the better side of him one last time, the side that he shows when he's unconscious. A member of the changeling cult, this bastard was so convinced of his coming immortality that he had a habit of making children with abandon. You don't know how many siblings you should have, probably a lot, but you've met none of them. Your arm is broken, that'll take a few days. At least most of the blood on the ground of your small shack built behind  the Xanaran's isn't yours. You walk outside and meet the creature who gave you the dagger. A small, bearded gnome... though, more skinny somehow, like a human or an elf, with four long fly wings behind him, and a jaw like a crab or a bug, looks in at the corpse of your father. 

"Can I have the rest of that?"

**The Neriadniclu, Archfey of Beginnings and Endings**
	
	**On why they helped**
		Fey business. Of course. My people always like to see our bargains kept. So now we can work on how you can repay me for that knife.
	**On how to repay**
		By entering another bargain. I can make you have never known this man, to have a large family, give you a healthier body and good magic, let you show your face to the world good and true without fear of it as much as a changeling can be, should that be your choice. 
	**What sort of family**
		Six siblings, and at least one uncle at the breakfast table, two parents, more when feast times come. They won't be changelings, but they won't all be the same either.
