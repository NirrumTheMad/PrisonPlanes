Originally, it was just a fever that never broke, a weak body that never worked, and a father who never gave up on seeking help for his daughter, who had to be quarantined to prevent the infection from spreading, who gave up so much of the family's funding to keep you alive. What father wouldn't?

It was easy enough for the Neriadniclu to find the whispers fey who infected you, the Terror fey who kept you sick and kept your room dusty, to find the swords fey that made your symptoms rocket erratically so you could feel more things, the glamour fey who drifted the scent of open ocean across your nose. Between he and the Pipperizzer, a small happening could occur. Ah, the pipperizzer, such a kind hearted fey, he gets more favour than he could ever spend from the children he rescues //before// they're in trouble, and goodness knows you, you weak, small, sick child, with no chance of being anything was a great place to start. 

Goodness, that time your father nearly bankrupted himself getting a resurrection for you, that was harsh. It is interesting that it was the result of having eaten so many Horditordi pears at once, the tummy ache you awoke with was horrid. He blamed it on the fact that you were outside for too long. He was never sure how you got out and got so far, you'd never really been able to walk that much before. Of course, that was the easiest place to leave you after you went away.  And of course, he never had the money to resurrect you, the more than four hundred gold pieces that Manarahi merchant was willing to spend on a trinket was just luck, wasn't it?

One month after that awful night, everything changed. Your fever burned with a potent magic, you had never //been// the sickly little girl who couldn't walk and whose hide was more worm than skin. Your memories only spoke of an arrogant girl who made most people around her uncomfortable, who boiled the seas just by existing. A fever, suddenly shared... as if nothing had ever changed.

But before you became you, you were a small, lost girl who swam out through a hole made by a tiny bearded thing with legs like a frogfish. 


"This is the beginning and the end for you, dreamer. If you don't act now, the fever will kill you"

**The neriadniclu, archfey of beginnings and endings**

	**What do I need to do?**
		First You'll need to eat these. These are, well I guess you'd call them Horditordi pears. Some Call them Gamblebrumbus fruit. Then you'll need to wish with all of your heart to have never been sick. Then, you're going to need to do something a little naughty.
			The weed-like creature holds out ten nuts, like little snails, they are small.
	**Naughty Deeds**
		A pretty fairy says they want you to give yourself a big gift, and if you can do that, you won't be sick anymore. I'm going to bring you to a big house, and you're going to need to find a special room in it. From there, you'll pick a present for yourself, but the people in the house don't realise that you having this present is good for them too, we're going to get you in so they can't find you. There's a special <MAGIC ITEM> that's yours, it was supposed to be yours from the beginning, it has your name on it.
	**Then What**
		Then you'll die, but you'll come back, and the item will be hidden for another you to find, and you won't have to hurt from this disease anymore.
