**Keep the throne? Who are you**
	"I am Captain Ma, currently the only living person to have punched my Father in his smug, heartless face, and Provided I deliver this message //and// you don't die, I have been promised the throne of Kholira, the Title of Godking, and control of of its people, armies, and resources."
			His hand glows a little as he makes a fist.
		"I'm a bit miffed I won't get to do it again, but he doesn't let me come close anymore" 

**Why were you named?**
	"I was the one that suggested he give up, set his people free from their service, end these wars, release the slaves, and put someone who gives a damn about them on the throne instead of letting Serascetimi and Parrabet turn the valley into a crater as all of his more competent children get slaughtered by those fortunate enough to be somehow immortal. I'm a bit surprised he passed over Gai, but I think chosing him would make it obvious he was planning something."
	
	
**Moseie's Plans**
	"He fears irrelevance, not death. I've watched him count the stars he's outlived, speak with beach sand about how long since it was a shell, and I've seen which of my aunts he's actually the most eager for. He thinks he can make a gravestone that'll last, or a story that'll be told, or a song that can't be unheard. Given his research, it's probably something magical.  A welcome mat for new gods, a Vaunted place in his own son's Cthonic halls, or some way to kill Haskill."
