====== Moonfall ======
Created Wednesday 16 October 2024

The moon follows several rules.

	* It is predictable because it runs from the sun which is controlled by druids such as the godking and the whales
	* It is fueled by divinity which it generates itself, simply by nature of being so large.
	* It is largely immune to Arcane(wizard) magic (with exceptions) and Physical attacks.
		* Those exceptions include 
			* Acid  and Radiant damage
	* It has weird interactions with the following spells
		* Bones of the earth -damage
		* Antipathy/Sympathy - Stun
		* Aura of Purity - disadvantage
		* Barkskin - blind
		* Commune with Nature - 

The defeat of the moon
