====== Eighth Floor - Novel ======
Created Thursday 05 September 2024

A continuous warm flame dances in a fireplace, and bright limelight bounces off of lamps on the wall, seemingly without any pipe to feed them. A modest number of books line the walls of the tavern-sized room, with some creating secluded spaces around chairs and tables.
