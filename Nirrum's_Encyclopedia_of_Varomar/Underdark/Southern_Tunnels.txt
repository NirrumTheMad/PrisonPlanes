====== Southern Tunnels ======
Created Monday 06 May 2024

The southern tunnels that run under the desert, Badlands, Saman, Ganglgrove, Drakenhearth, and Jiannok Sio are arguably the most peaceful part of the underdark and indeed, much of the plane.Largely disconnected from the prison, the environmental effects here are strong. Frequent pockets of water, acid, magma, and high-pressure airlocks in between them.

The few creatures that survive this mistreatment are either extremely dangerous or more commonly, rather benign extremphiles. With a Few of the right spells and the right magic items, this can reliably be the safest area on the plane to travel through.
