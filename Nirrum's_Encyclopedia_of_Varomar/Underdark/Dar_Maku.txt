====== Dar Maku ======
Created Monday 06 May 2024

Even Outside of the cracks, the land shattered, but where it didn't flood in the south or fill with lava in the north, many even older tunnels to the prison remained generally intact. This band runs along the gannaga until tornitur (with some small ones that travel past that toward kriegcove), along the grass road, even hitting the very tip of Mohara and the Saman plains, and even extends up into the province of roses, where it is interrupted by the inferno.  

Here, ore is plentiful and predictable, though most of the tunnels that are not wormbore (which is relatively rare because of how the strata is arranged) go straight to the underground sea and the prison, meaning that incursions from these tunnels tend to be worse for people, and excursions into those tunnels can quickly turn deadly. 

The other most common form of tunnel here is abandoned dwarf fortresses, mostly of the lelumadil. while some get reclaimed from time to time, the dwarves feel the safest in their dear Skalmirthon mountainhome.
