====== The Cracks ======
Created Friday 15 March 2024

Caused by the shearing of continental Varomar, the cracks are a series of  deep fissures caused by the stresses of both the cataclysm and the violent ejection of the intraegian portion of the magma sea. 

The fissures are horrifying, often featuring a honeycomb of hill-sized flakes that never settled fully sitting vertically atop eachother, supported by a wall of what is essentially volcanic glasses, as well as their own stress-welded selves. The pits descend hundreds of feet,  and are not infrequently terminated by the walls of magma chambers or creeping toward the underground sea. This means that all along the edges of the aegis mountains, there are hot spots that produce hot springs, sulfur vents, and geysers, but also pitfall caves that are often only thinly spanned by tenacious plant life or the overlapping cones of the outer aegis ranges.

The cracks rapidly produced the ideal conditions for many forms of natural caves, cycling extremely hot and  acidic water against mixed strata in some places, building steam pressure and natural gasses in others. 

Dwarven tunnels are the most reliable crossings over the cracks in the west. The southern cracks are nearly impassable, and the eastern cracks are some of the most dangerouns, as there are more decents into the prison there than anywhere else on the plane.
