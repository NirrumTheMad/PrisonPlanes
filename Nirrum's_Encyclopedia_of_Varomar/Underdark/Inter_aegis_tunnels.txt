====== Inter aegis tunnels ======
Created Friday 15 March 2024

Nearly exclusively artificial tunnels bored through what is largely rhyolite, save where the fissures of the cracks got particularly out of hand such as in the eastern aegis.

Of course, in the western aegis, the Dwarven mines of Skalmirthon reign over all other tunnels on the plane. Ordered and solid, save where they encroach on the edges of the underground sea, from which many monsters emerge.

Wormbore and gnome tunnels run their course as well, especially in the south and east, both supporting their own dangers.
