# Introduction 
Created Saturday 25 January 2025

Time and time again, HE WHO WAS THE FIRST, said the words "Hey, welcome back"

HeHoWaTheFi had again saved his world from complete annihilation at the hands of a being that ate time. 

Each event, he was ignored when he remade time. Every power structure simply took his good will and turned it on him... only to beg once it happened again.

Until one time, when things were different. 

He freed first those who would work for him, and slowly rebuilt the world, so that people now needed to *purchase* their providential, preferential retemporality.

This time "You'll have to sign this paper and pay me to do this again, or I will leave you frozen until you are the last person to free. Your competitors will all have an advantage over you, if we get to you at all"

Well that didn't sit well with a lot of people, and it didn't sit well with them for an extremely long time, until, as promised they were freed after everyone else was, left with nothing. "You should have signed on to the Tajik Corporation's Retemporal Insurance Roster. The others were freed preferrentially based on how much they were able to put towards their plan!"


They paid, they kept paying, they bought the secrets that would let them self-start, and then, when it didn't work, they payed again. A few got successful a few times, and instead of Rebuff from the Tajik Corporation they found themselves in rare, unique positions where the corporation payed //them... with the money they had payed it.//

But time and time again, They were bailed out by the Tajik Corporation. 

Much time has passed, for most things. Many things died, many things were reborn, but the Tajik corporation kept researching, kept puttering away at new technologies, new weapons, to fight back the end of all time.



This time, it's your turn.
