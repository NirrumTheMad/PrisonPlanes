# 5e Conversion 
Created Saturday 01 February 2025

To begin, the power scale of this world is within the realms that many games of exalted play at. A level three character in Tajik is the equivalent of a Fully kitted out level 20 character in most worlds.

Your cantrips the equivalent of spell masteries
Your Action surge, the equivalent of three,
Your attack, the equivalent of all attacks you could manage
Your Damage, each point the equivalent of a die
Your meager invocations, the seeming whispers of greater powers, themselves the results of things that eclipse most of what those powers were.
You may build your form entirely from scratch, provided you cast detect balance upon it.

Conveniently, this all works out the same, but starting at level 3 is about the lowest you can go.

### Skills 
Proficiency or Deficiency
Expertise or Annihilation 
Skills can now be added to or subtracted from.


Acrobatics Now allows you to hover A number of feet per turn equal to your bonus, still using your movement, falling if you do not end your turn on a surface you could normally move on. A Negative Bonus reduces forced movement by 5x that amount. If the reduced movement amount is Equal to more than half your speed, you cannot be proned while conscious 
Animal Handling, now just **Handling,** allows you to hypnotise creatures with a CR up to your bonus. A Negative bonus becomes the equivalent bonus to saving throws against Mind-Altering Effects
Arcana is the only Intelligence knowledge Skill Remaining. You Automatically understand all technologies and Identify all effects equal to (or less than) your Bonus, at least partly. A negative bonus can add to investigation checks if secrets are present.
	I have an app on my phone that Idenitifies plants, Locates all significant Astrological phenomena, and tells me if I can eat Borgar. This skill is essentially your prowess at search engines. 
Athletics works as usual and you use your bonus for all strength checks. Negative athletics adds to stealth checks in crowds
Deception works as usual. Negative deception reduces propaganda attempts by the same amount
	Propaganda is something that world imposes upon you, or does about you.
**History has been replaced with Course**. Course allows you to add your bonus to your attempts to retime something, and resist being timed-out
	Course is the understanding of entropy, and how to speed it up. 
Insight Functions as Usual, though can now function at degrees of separation up to one quarter of your bonus, rounded down. Negative Insight is added to attempts to avoid Arcane Traps.
	This includes both many magical effects and doomscrolling
Intimidation now allows you to frighten creatures of your choice with a CR equal to your bonus as a bonus action within a number of feet equal to 5x your level. A negative bonus adds to persuasion attempts to reduce hostility
Investigation works as usual. You Automatically Locate all clues with level up to your bonus. Negative investigation allows you to speak an additional language per point
Medicine contains its original effects, and you can cause a creature you touch to gain one hit point as a bonus action a number of times a day equal to your bonus. Negative medicine adds to your attempts to resist //Exodegeneration//
	Exodegeneration is an effect of many Forbidden creatures that feeds on your understanding of yourself to induce changes.
**Nature is now replaced with Dharma. **Dharma allows you to add your bonus to death saving throws, and reduces resurrection costs by 100x your bonus (spells whose material components are reduced to 0 this way do not require material components to work on you).
**Perception** Functions as usual, though at distances multiplied by your bonus. Negative Perception allows you to percieve additional planes up to your bonus, within which you can make perception checks without adding your perception bonus
	Network Traffic (Craving)
	Ethereal plane (Restlessness)
	Ley (Doubt)
	Local afterlives (Anger)
	Time (Sloth)
**Performance** allows you to (as a bonus action) be heard or seen clearly at normal distances multiplied by your bonus (requires concentration). A negative perfomance prevents perceptions of you by being effected by illusions and enchantments that others are under the effects of, with levels up to your bonus
Persuasion functions as usual. Negative Persuasion adds to Network Stealth checks. 
**Religion is now replaced with Ritual.** Ritual allows you to prepare a number of extra ritual spells numbering up to your bonus each day. A spell prepared this way cannot exceed half your level
Sleight of hand functions as normal. Your sleight of hand bonus also applies Mechanical checks. Negative  Sleight of Hand increases the value of your currency by an amount equal to your bonus (per denomination)
	Password Management, Warding rites. 
Stealth functions as normal, and you able to supress your presence inversely proportionate to your bonus as a bonus action (requires concentration). Negative Stealth adds to checks made to distract.
**Survival** functions as normal and you are now able to apply your skills to timespans and distances that are proportionate to your bonus. A Negative Survival adds to your ability to appraise the value of goods.

### **Levelling description** 
	There are choices to be made for each ability to describe how it would manifest in this world
		Temporally - The action is faster, even broaching the realms of impossibility in lesser worlds. 
		Spatially - the action is bigger, or the implement changes shape to suit the moment
		Physiologically - you personally change to be able to perform the action. Extra arms, Heads, wings, mouths, eyes. You are able to manifest these at will
		Arcanotechnologically - you have modified yourself or the situation in a way that allows the action to happen as described, you'll have to do it again every time, by magic or machine, but at least you can. 
			Summoning the dead
			Creating automata
			Blastercasting etc;
		And many others

When you level as a cleric, you are no longer communing with just your old god, but the chorus of the godhead in which your god's voice can be heard. Hundreds of Thousands of gods with similar ideals, all lending you a fraction of their divinity. You very likely outrank your god within a few levels.
