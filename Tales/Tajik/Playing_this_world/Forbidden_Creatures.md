# Forbidden Creatures 
Created Monday 24 February 2025

At the edge of infinity, is a wall-front of awful things, the unfulfilled wishes of a universe trying to make sense. Occasionally, in the writhing of their wall, they find a name, and from name, they find form, and drop from the edge of the universe into the skies of Tajik, where they crawl out from the wastes, through the cracks of dreams, from droplets of blood or shattered glass. This is called **The Wall Where Angels Do Not Walk, Fields of Broken Seeds,  Colour that the Heavens Refuse to See, Infinite world of Writhing Karma, End of all Intent. **Or, rather simply, the wall. 



Grace Eaters
	Like starfish, or hands, but bigger, darker, and horrifying

Sharper 
	A form of creature that sticks still in dark places, making objects fall over and things break
	
Slyther
	A worm with three tails attached to a disc body, things that it touches slowly turn into a copies of itself. It numbs creatures, mutes electronic signals, and is very sticky. It is exceptional at running away but a very persistent predator once it has found suitable prey. It is also extremely hard to kill, and the most time-efficient way to kill them is launch them back at the wall

Gollar
	A gnome-like creature that can only be dispelled by lightning. It screeches if it is found, eats magic, and likes to blight victims that it stalks

Insinuator
	The invariable fate of most of the ascended elf lords from many many worlds. Too long staring at the wall has changed this creature into the beginnings of something mad. They often carry great insight to the universe at large and aren't particularly violent, but they are often seen just before everyone in a town turns into forbidden creatures themselves
	
Neurovore
	A crawling scaly thing with a tail. It is suspected that these are what happens to those corrupted by The Wall. They  are blind but have the ability to feel vibrations. They eat the nerves of most creatures.


Sheehom
	The remains of Angels or Avens or Birdfolk, it is not quite clear, but they travel in groups. They have red cone like faces filled with holes, but feed through their arms, from a hole in the middle of which extends their proboscis to drink the fluids and life of whatever they find. They also have a habit of mimicking voices
	
Braatus
	At some point, the wall encountered what a dwarf was. This is an echo of that dwarf, somehow a part of the endless expanse of the wall
	
Goom 
	Many shapes on the wall are capable of reflection. Not Self reflection, usually, but often capable of mimmicking the thoughts, or some significant part of a creature's mind back to them. Goom are often creatures that will hypnotise and control a creature, eventually consuming them by becoming their memories.
Asha
	soft falling flakes that are poisonous to the touch. Creatures that die to them become Asha, blowing away on the wind into millions of tiny creatures
	
Buvodimo
	Corals that absorb all that they touch, turning them into their own spreading fortress, protecting their vascular cores.

Babble Tarcher
	Small, circular creatures, like a coin of meat with four to nine legs, that slowly change a creature's linguistic abilities and communication methods. Isolating them socially is the first step to degenerating their minds entirely, only to beat them into a pulp when they can't fight back, nesting in the remains.
	* Message the player with a rewritten sentence on occasion, slowly progress to not being able to understand things. Occasionally tell other players what they hear come out of the targetted player's mouth

Tyïka
	Precursors of creatures like Mind-flayers. Tyïka are usually humanoid creatures that swim in the wall. They are immensely intelligent but also paranoid to an extreme degree. They pit other wall-creatures against eachother, usually living inside of harder ones. Their most notable ability is their capability to attach fake sentiences to objects or other creatures to lure forbidden creatures to them
