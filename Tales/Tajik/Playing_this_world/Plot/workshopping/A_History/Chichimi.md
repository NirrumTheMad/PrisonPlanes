# Chichimi 
Created Wednesday 28 May 2025

A rather harsh tropical desert occupies the northern half of chichimi, while the southern half is dense tropical jungle. The desert is home to Eishur, An absolutely gargantuan shield volcano, and the tallest point on the plane. The Roots of this mountain stretch below what is currently sea level, and its slope is extremely gentle. 

Connected now by a fairly shallow gorge is Chichiwa, once a large island nation.
