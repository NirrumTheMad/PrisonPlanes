# Hemiha Ayi 
Created Wednesday 21 May 2025

Hemiha Ayi was once a large continent on the northern Hemisphere with deep fjords, where shortly after a glacial maximum, an intense global warming period caused the glaciers to melt and carve huge paths rather quickly. Bordered by Ali north Ocean. 

Cities on Hemiha Ayi include:

Hemma

Ūbanjūm
