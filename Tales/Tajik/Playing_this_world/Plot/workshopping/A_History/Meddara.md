# Meddara 
Created Wednesday 28 May 2025

	The Largest continent, Somewhat triangular in shape. Extending from the antarctic circle to the middle of the northern tropics. Its northern tip is currently an extremely rainy earthquake ridden, volcano-plagued, storm-cursed mudpit, as the equitorial plate is being forced under Chichimi. The middle of Meddara's Temperate region is occupied by Èsukono, the second highest mountain in the world.
