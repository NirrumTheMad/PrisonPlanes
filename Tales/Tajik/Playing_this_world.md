# Playing this world 
Created Thursday 23 January 2025

It is hard to say what sort of system this world can be played with
[[+5e Conversion]]
The world outside of towns is lifeless, still, liminal and subliminal. The world inside of towns is ruthless, and uncaring towards itself, but charity exists in deeply powerful pockets, both spatially and metaphorically.

A character is agent in that they are capable of emulating some sort of disaster, as each theme is intended to be a theme of power through decay.
A Character is patient in that they are bound by a society that clings heavily to its themes, and is both hard to move and hard to stop
A character is challenged in that they are beset by rushing enemies on all fronts
A character is aided, in that failure is always temporary. Someone can and will find your timeless body and revive you //eventually//.
What you will lose in that time is up to fate

**Player**
	It is expected that players are not simply flesh and blood, though it is not forbidden. Machine, Magic, Metaphor, and Meat are all welcome in design.
	Humans are one of the strangest shapes you can take, and within that mindset, they are not rare.
	
	Every single person is required to have a sealing dagger on them at all times. Even the meagrest of attempts to close a rift can buy precious seconds to save centuries. They are free, they are ubiquitous, some people carry multiple.
	
	Power on Tajik is immense. Regardless of your abilities in other worlds, what it takes to accomplish anything in this world is *titanic*. To be restful, a chair must be more than a flat piece to sit on. The diseases that plague this world require more than a simple bandaid or tooth brush to get rid of.
