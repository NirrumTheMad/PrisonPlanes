Clara's Sea Elf

You awaken in the draftiest room in ShLu-guum-tk-tktktk. It is dusty, filled with the detritus of the perpetually falling organisms from the surface. It has to be, of course. You father, a hardshaper of midling talent has not called you for breakfast, he hasn't in many years. Your mother, A water-bearing racer, died just over three years ago. You are the oldest of five siblings, and it was a great relief that you decided to take an outer room on the home, and give up your room to your less-cursed siblings. Cursed. As you open your eyes... When did you fall asleep? You don't do that often, but you confirm that yes, you are still perpetually boiling the water around you. You don the mask your mother comissioned from the grao, made of something from the dry continent, and it directs the bubbles away from your eyes, up to the hole in the cieling where it escapes, drawing cold, dusty water in from outside. Thankfully, you've never had to care about the parasites that try to float in and burrow into you, They can't stand the heat. Your siblings on the other hand, probably do. They love you, but... the rumble of the bubbling is hard to hear over, The cracks in the cieling take time and energy for them to mend, a rare boon to your father, as your siblings are *all* hard-shapers. You as well of course, but your curse makes it difficult to work sometimes. You have spent so much time trying to understand your curse that you've come to recognise its perks. For every broken roof, a bounty in shrimp caught in the curtain of bubbles. For every hour spent sitting in a pot boiling the water to salt, an unending supply of clean water. For every suggestion that maybe you should go travelling, a few warm thank-yous.
You are certainly not useless, but after getting used to your value, it is hard for even those closest to ignore the inconviences, Cracked roofs, parasites, bleached furniture, uncomfortable warmth. You sit up as you hear a clamour outside, The rungagung of the bell, signifying the arrival of the Grao for trade.

Your house is, of course, one of the outer shells. A lump on a massive cluster of egg-like protrusions, your town mostly contained within, with a few megaosteoic Barnacles the size of three buildings on their own to protect from some larger threats
Your father is securing a pack-crate, larger than he is, to his back in the main room. There is a plate of grouper, always the big meals for your family and... Molasses?

Your father says it is a special day. The titan, South DustyCurrent, Rainstarter, Windriser is nearby, the Grao are in. He asks if you are going to go see the titan.

South DustyCurrent, Rainstarter, Windriser, among many epithets, is a sort of kindred spirit, hero, something. He also makes the water warm, and no one asks *him* to leave unless it's going to start a war.

If you decide to go, you will have to wait in line.

From here, the player can either go meet the grao, or S-DC,R,W.
