The_God_King

Created Sunday 03 June 2018

The God-King of Kholira, Currently the 14th, Is the title taken by the most powerful druid in Kholira. It is usually hereditary, but there was a coup during the reign of the fourth god king, that lead to the current dynasty.

The fourteenth and current Godking, named Moseie, is a Half-elf Kholirahi, the other half being so many things. His Skin is deep sky blue, his eyes a vibrant orange. His hair is Black and slightly long, though not to his shoulders. He looks about 26, he is actually just over two hundred. He keeps a short full beard along a very cut jaw (though he frequently changes the style), and stands at a proud 5'11. His ears are short in comparison to many half-elves. He wears black pants embroidered with sliver vines, but other than that, wears nothing else but his crown and Staff. The roots under his skin are white, And as of 5853 streaked with red. His crown is made of silver, inset with polished wood spheres. It is small and not particularly ornate. His staff is crimson, with a gold ferrule on the bottom and a transparent globe on top containing what appears to be a small bluish star.

Moseie intends to take over the world, as the previous nine did before him. He believes his powers are more than god-given, that it is natural law that he is who he is, and there doesn't seem to be much evidence to the contrary. The god-king is born into power from those who have power, then earns power, then takes power. All of his power is a natural consequence to his actions and the actions of those before him. His intent beyond that power is to destroy the will of all creatures below him, to clear their minds so that he may speak and they will only hear his words, not the constant squabble of people who know nothing. Only then, when they hear him, we he lift them into the stars, Everyone a god-king born, and himself, likely far beyond that. He is not a fool, and knows that he cannot yet take on the entire world himself. He is a master networker and tactician, likely only beaten by Orsithach furi in the former, and by none in the latter. His Armies move at his command, and those of his Fatal Generals. These generals are themselves masters of tactics, but mostly of interpreting the god-kings wishes. Two of them are his own sons, one weilding an artifact that got Moseie contacts with the cult of Teruna. To the Moseie, this was the greatest display of loyalty that a father could ever recieve from his son, especially given the nature of the relationship between himself and the thirteen previous god-kings, and was one of the only times the god-king has ever cried. The more loyal citizens cried as well, for such a move meant stability for their people and was of course rather poetic.

Moseie spends his time out in the god-king's garden which never seems to have snow build up on it. There, he listens to the cosmos, watching their dance as he slowly works his way to being less insignificant. He enjoys his consorts, and being a father. It can even be said that other than the permanent and imminent threat of fratricide that he gives to each new son, he's a rather good father. He spends his time with his children and knows all their names, the names of their mothers, and takes raising them seriously. He knows one day they might try to replace him, but he has made that transition harder than any god-king before him. He has had to kill eleven of his own ambitious sons and even one of his daughters and a score of his consorts when they attempted to take the throne. He has never felt remorse for those deaths, nor will he, he had to kill eighty nine of his brothers, most of them immortals with over a century of experience. He also feels no remorse for the Atrocities he not only must, but intends to commit in order to gain the power he seeks. He is friendly and fairly approachable despite his goals. He knows not everyone can be as powerful as he is yet, and seeks the value of all opportunities.
Moseie is not particularly forgiving of failure, though he is scrutinous in his punishments or in his delegations, a punishment never exceeds the crime.

You can Imagine why the university is still in Manaharamu. What a lunatic. Not to mention his atrocities to his unwilling consorts.

Moseie is not greedy, He expects to be killed by his sons, he is attached to his own legacy, afraid that he will leave Kholira without being its greatest Godking, Afraid that the nation that made him one that he truly fears will overwrite is existence. He is also afraid of the dark, which is why he keeps his staff with him at all times, and in part loathes Kholira for its Dark winter nights and the dramatisation of the godking as someone who must dwell in the night. He expresses this fear in feigned ambivalence, masking an indifferent cruelty. Moseie has been assaulted by dreams of his death since he was a very small child. Perhaps, it is some measure of the power of the happening, peering through time that has given him such a clear roadmap of his life. He fights it, actively, using so many tricks to ward off eldritch assault that it is a surprise of his court to see him struggle, to show fear. This angers him, he will fix that later. None can even tell what he is doing and he looks like a fool. He drags down a comet into the castle to illustrate his struggle. Three of his generals know this is fake. Two of them understand something more is going on. A little over half of the room is convinced.

When Moseie was young, About seven, his mother woke him. Kholira was under attack. A flight of dragons, non-natives that saw that valley of war and the ualakomara as a good place to subjugate. He was to go down into the chambers below Kholira, where their secrets are kept, where the Godking's greatest asset waits. He did so, and he waited, and waited, there with his mother while his father, his older brothers, his few remaining uncles and aunts, all struck against those dragons. It should have been an easy win, But two hundred years ago, Kholira was weaker. Moseie wasn't king. His Father's name is barely spoken of in Kholira, part because Moseie has outlived most of the Country's residents, partly because his father was entirely unnoteworthy, and were it not for the power of one of his older brothers, The country would have fallen there. His father ruled the rest of his days as a hollow shell, his mother was never quite good at raising a child. She tried her best, but her own attachment and desire for status meant that Moseie was a secondary concern. Had things been different, this alone would have precluded Moseie from the godking's throne. He was treated with ire, irreverence, cruelty, and disrespect by many of the others in the castle. He came to think of this as normal

But either by fey working or stoic shadowfell magic, Moseie's path was pushed in a new direction. He had to fight, for years thereafter for recognition, against his brothers, against the generals, against all of kholira it seemed. By Fey working or stoic shadowfell magic, There was little compassion in Kholira, for a boy who just wanted attention. Made all the worse by the numbing of the roots, the death of the wise in the dragon's attack.

He wouldn't have it. He was ten when he left to become strong. When he returned, twelve years later, he was strong. His father recovered slightly over the decades and had been gathering magical weapons and research to continue the Kholirahi volksgeist dream of taking over snarl. His return home was unremarkable. His father died in relative obscurity , complacency, as Moseie would think it, within a week of his return. When his brothers all began their fight for the throne, it was not too difficult of a battle. Many of them had great and powerful gifts but not a single one of them had undergone the Journey Moseie had. Through hell, heaven, all around the dry lands, drifting through the oceans and rivers, seeing much of Snarl and some of what lies beyond. It was his first taste at notoriety, when he slew his Aasimar brother, a veritable angel, without even really realizing who he was. During this 9th ascension war, Moseie wasn't even spoken of as a contender. No one had heard of his deeds, and he was considered a wayward son until he trounced all competition, almost completely unaware of the names and legacies of the people he fought. Moseie will forever be proud of not knowing all of his achievements, the reasons he causes Awe in people. It just comes naturally to him.

But. He is afraid of that. He has grown attached to his position as Godking, not just because of the luxuries, but because it means that there are so very few on snarl who don't know who he is. He Never quite recovered from some of his ordeals and has trauma around certain types of darkness. The night Sky does not scare him, but his own bedroom glows a very dim peach colour. The rare partner that he keeps with him for long gets used to occasional nightmares. He often pushes them away because he cannot bear people to know his weakness. To his immense credit, he has not completely lost his ability to view other people as people. He has never forgotten a name or a face since he was very young, and treats children with immense respect and compassion, even disregarding his station and experience. Anyone who has reached the barest scrap of adulthood, however, gets a very cold shoulder, one of complete indifference to struggles.





As of 5853, The godking has gained the ability to use the Ancient Red Dragon's Breath Weapon once between long rests

It is far easier to list the considerations one needs to take into account should one encounter the godking here, rather than listing them all in a Stat block.

  • Sillowmin's Boon (additional level of exhaustion, expertise in Arcana, Ylla Sillowmin)
  • Unconcerning aspect (1/day, creatures cannot attempt to actively percieve him until he acts harmfully, stealth vs passive perception only, can't be identified as an individual in crowds of ten+ creatures)(fey, the zon)
  • full colour vision, ranging into the mircowave and x-ray spectrums involuntarily. (complex origin, fey and shadowfell)
  • A 100ft radius pulse of colour that reveals invisible creatures (the gaesial)
  • Immunity to fire damage, fire breath (contract with The world tree)
  • Capable of replacing fire damage with lightning, all spells that deal fire ignore resistance (Boon of fire soul)
  • 12 level magic capability
  • Advance Chronoception (The Skillogintan)
  • Eye of the basilisk, Perfect Stillness, Deafening silence
  • regen 1d6 while conscious
  • Free casts of: Earthquake, Flight, cause fear
  • Great Gathering Ritual
  • Wildshape double CR, Additional use
  • Seven of the eight steps


The god king is no stranger to combat, especially not from powerful enemies, he takes a bit of joy from a good challenge. He uses his rudmagic wantonly, and relishes the chance to talk with his opponent and put them to his use. His staff of stars literally channels the power of a star and is volatile. He uses it to cast but mostly so he can look good while casting, the roots under his skin are really all he needs. He is Directly attuned to the living magic of The Great White World Tree Treysa.
