	<h1>Temple_of_the_World_Spirit <a name='Nirrum's Encyclopedia of Varomar:Ctutoletl:Temple of the World Spirit'></a></h1>
	</div>

	<div class='content'>
	<p>
Created Friday 05 October 2018<br>
<a href="./Temple_of_the_World_Spirit/How_it_went_for_me.html" title="+How_it_went_for_me" class="page">+How_it_went_for_me</a> 
</p>

<p>
The entrance to the world spirit's temple is at the top. A huge bronze bell needs to ring to unlock the path inward. Underneath the bell is signs of a fire being lit such as a blackned bowl-shaped depression, some ashes and unburnt wood. A small campfire burning for ten minutes will prepare the bell, allowing to be rung while it is still hot, the bell must be heated evenly, so one cast of heat metal won't be able to emulate the effects, though three sustained casts would. The bell rings for an unnaturally long time before anything happens. Quite suddenly, the central square block, containing the bell and the fire both drop downward, some parts of the block stopping earlier than others, making a narrow staircase that descends 100 feet before stopping. Ten Minutes later, the entrance resets back to normal.<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
<b>Dm's Note:</b> As an added bonus, the players have actually just crossed over into the fey lands. If you want to do some time-warp shenanigans, wether to catch them up with another group or delay them until another group catches up, this is a great place for some timeline changes. <b>Torches that aren't part of the dungeon emit green fire and a low-pitched hum </b>
</div>
</p>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The Purpose of the dungeon is for the world-spirit to communicate with the party the importance of safeguarding the bones against their destruction, thus, the world's destruction and the Freeing of Teruna. Thus The theme of this dungeons is <i>Broken Prisons. </i><b>The world spirit itself is trying to communicate its intent with them. This entire place was done with spirit magic. Creatures that die here find themselves in the Xanaran's at the end of the dungeon a few minutes later and are ushered into the stinger-opening without triggering the end scene.</b>
</div>
</p>

<br>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
<i>This dungeon does not reset in any way, it was intended for the people who are now entering it. If they get clever and start breaking shit. Let'em. If they tunnel out of the dungeon, they're in fey lands. Good luck with that.</i>
</div>
</p>

<br>

<p>
 DUNGEON STYLE<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The dungeon is in the shape of a Scorpion with a Snakelike tail as if it were standing on a Dodecahedron (a d12). The tail is of a narratively infinite length but eventually pierces the hall of concept where it comes into contact with the claw Trochanters<br>
The Dungeon's Colors are black, pale green, Gold,  and pale pink. 
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
It smells unnotable unless otherwise noted. The shadows are soft and the light is diffuse, unless otherwise noted. Sound echoes hard but poorly, the acoustics are terrible. 
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The walls and cieling qualify as rather cheap but still striking semi-precious gems. The Doors are of magical quality and are immutable
</div>
</p>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The dungeon has <b>black doors</b> inlayed with gold filligree in the shape of a poem in abyssal, describing how the world-speaker should stay out of the way of the SNAKE FOLK. The words are charged with magic that prevent the door from being damaged while it is in the fey lands. The doors are large and their frames are hexagonal. They have no hinges but a handle on one side allows the door to be opened by rolling it sideways. Opening doors requires an  <b>action</b> with a SKILL contest  slightly higher than average roll.  The doors are well-balanced but are mechanically Driven to close. Keeping them open for more than one round causes the SKILL contest  to increase by about  a quarter for every round that passes up to a maximum of 150% reasonable, If the door is held open past this point, it breaks and remains open. Undoing the Door's Magic requires two rolls, a SKILL contest  a bit higher than 4/4 reasonableness of UNDERSTANDING MAGIC to learn how to pause the magic and a SKILL contest  3/4 HOLD YOUR HAND STEADY to scratch the appropriate lines. The doors feel glassy with obvious changes in texture where the writing is. 
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
<b>DM's Note: </b><i>The Doors close with enough force to bend steel and crush many forms of rock, but even that has its limits</i><br>
<i>Nirrum's Note: I can't help but notice that it is only the doors with these protections... I've got a few dwarves in mind for this dungeon</i>
</div>
</p>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The Dungeon has Green Serpentine (Of course) Walls, with snakes in relief. The walls seem to smell of blood, or copper but none is present. The walls are nearly a meter thick at least. (<b>but not indestructible if someone takes a pick to it, go to 20 if they escape</b>). The walls feel glassy in all places where the carving has been finished, though occasional imperfections in the material will create a void or a small rough spot<br>
The Dungeon is lit by wall mounted sconces of magical light. This light is sunlight. The sconces are shaped like Snakes which are trying to eat the source of the light. 
</div>
</p>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The floors are pink Rhodonite. They are polished to Reflective. Below them is some extremely plastic but curiously mixed igneous bedrock, except where noted.
</div>
</p>

<p>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The cielings are also rhodonite are are the same thickness as the walls unless otherwise noted and tend to be 15ft high, though the polish is far less clean
</div>
</p>

<br>
<br>

<p>
1) Antechamber<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The first room of the world-speaker's temple only despicts a mural of The World Spirit . There is a door to the north(2), and unopenable doors to  east(6), and west(9). The way the party came in is a large pit that seals with a square pillar in two minutes. The pillar slides upward, taking the bell with it. The pillar is rough, worn smooth in some places by imperfect setting, but largely it remains made of the same granite the rest of the outside of the temple is.
</div>
</p>

<p>
2) Hall of Concept<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The hall of concept has four doors, North(9), East(3), West(6), and South(1). <br>
<b>A large, slightly curved Spike hangs from the cieling, extremely close inspection reveals a tiny hole in the tip. The cieling is a dome that is 50ft high at its peak, where the curved spike hangs. </b><br>
This room is actually <i>Unfinished. </i> The walls are partially uncarved and a table sits near the North Door. The room is a dome that is 80ft wide
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
 On the table is a small contraption. When a pin with a ring is pulled off of the contraption. The cheap string it was holding rises as a brass counterweight drops, pushing on a lever. There are two such pins and pulling on the second one lowers a second counterweight which pushes another lever, causing a tiny facsimile of a door to open on the contraption. The model can be picked up and interacted with but it is completely mundane.
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
Next to the table is a crate of dull chisels, awls, and punches. Next to that are eight broken hammer hafts. In front of the table is the skeleton of  a snake folk IMPORTANT ONE. A tarnished dagger is on the ground nearby, the first half of the dagger is rusted. <br>
The North Door(9) is unopenable, though it has a handle that would indicate that it <i>can </i>be opened. Two sconces flank the door making that part of the room exceptionally bright. The Door only opens when both have gone out<br>
The East(3) and West Doors(6) are both open
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
<b>Dm's Note:</b><i> This IMPORTANT SNAKE died when another SNAKE FOLK assassinated her. The IMPORTANT SNAKE FOLK had intimate knowledge about the dungeon</i>
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
When the players re-enter the room after releasing both pins, sliding down from the ceiling above the north door is a cage suspended by two thick chains. Inside there is a EATY SPOOKY MONSTER THAT IS OTHERWISE IMMORTAL. It  Breaks out and Chases the players and attempts to kill them. That said, if they try the now darkened North Door (9) it will open.
</div>
</p>

<br>

<p>
3) Left Trochanter and Femur<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
This room is decorated with a mosaic mural detailing the Timeline of the plane up to the cooling of the aegis and the return of life to the surface, and starts with the presence of pelor. The beginning of this room is a round section that is closed off with bars. Normal steel bars. Completely nonmagical and only capable of what steel bars reasonably can do. They are spaced 1 inch apart. The next 30ft is a long and flat section  which contains most of the mural. There is a trap in the long section. A pressure plate  near the middle which drops a 10x10x5 cage on whoever triggers it. The pressure plate does not take up the whole hallway, There is three feet on either side to allow people past. The person must make a surprise SKILL contest  for BEING FAST, 17/20 or be trapped within the cage, if the creature caught in the cage is taller than 5ft, it takes [up to a maximum of 4 times what a normal human can stand before going unconscious] as well. The cage requires a SKILL contest  9/10 STRONG to lift. The door at the end of the hallway leads to room (4), the left patella
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
<b>Dm's Note: </b><i>Choose your own trapped square but choose it before they enter the room, it is a SKILL contest  8/10 FIND CLUES to spot the pressure plate but it requires UNSCRUPULOUS INSTRUMENTS to deactivate without force</i>
</div>
</p>

<p>
4) Left Patella<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
This room is 30ft long, 10ft wide and Magically Darkened. The entire room is also covered in poisonous spikes which have poisonous barbs on them. The barbs are stationary but it is a  surprise SKILL contest  for BEING FAST 17/20<br>
throw to not be punctured and a SURPRISE SKILL contest  3/4 with HOLD YOUR LUNCH  to not be poisoned once you get  poked by either a barb or a full spike.The spikes do 1d6 damage, the Barbs do 1 damage, and the poison does 1d10 damage and the creature is SICK for one hour. There is a 2ft wide s-shaped Crawlway  that a creature can crawl or crouch along with ease if it is able to avoid the spikes. At 10ft, 20ft and 25ft, the are barbed rods going across the hallway that stop creatures from standing that have the same effect as the barbs in the rest of the room.
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
<b>Dm's Note: </b><i>It is not unreasonable to cast a decently high power spell, or have some magical sense that trivialises this challenge. That's intentional. </i>
</div>
</p>

<br>

<p>
5) The Left Claw<br>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The door to the left claw(6) from the Antechamber (1) is locked. It lacks a handle from that side.This room can only be entered from room (5). The Door to the Antechamber is one-way and only is able to be opened from this side and then, only after the Pin is pulled out. 
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
The Room Is in the shape of an emperor scorpion's claw. Entering the room, one sees a hallway that is mostly straight and thin to their right. The hallway's left wall is jagged and approximately 60ft long.  To the right there is a larger chamber, 65ft long, which has statues of an SMITH-THEMED CREATURE, a stormy giant, a human wizard and a Tree. The Tree is notably a real tree with exceptionally white bark. Its roots extend through the podium base and out into the bedrock beyond and strangely seem to get thicker the further they are followed. The statues are placed in irregularly sized alcoves on the right wall of this hallway. The right wall is deeply curved. althought he hallway ends  rather narrowly, like the opposite hall. At the end of this pincer, there is a Bottle on a pedastel which should be identifiable as a potion of healing.  In the deepest part of the curve, upon a small dias, there is a Pin made of Granite that is chained to a large Steel circle in the cieling. 
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
The pin is attached to a BIRD LIKE, VERY ANGRY MONSTER via a 5ft chain that it cannot escape. The FLYING ANGRY MONSTER is extremely hostile. Removing the pin causes the steel circle to extremely rapidly ascend 20ft, revealing a hole in the ceiling through which 8 quasits(<i>mm63)</i> climb out, while also Freeing the FLYING ANGRY MONSTER to move around.
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 60pt'>
<b>Dm's Note: </b><i>This should go without saying, but it is an easier fight if they kill the FLYING ANGRY MONSTER before taking up the pin. Punish them if they try to free it first. Focus down the kind-hearted, attack them first. The FLYING ANGRY MONSTER cannot get out itself. Magic. The demons just want to kill something, they don't care if they die.</i>
</div>
<div style='padding-left: 30pt'>
