The Dream of Rain is a god that recently landed in Tajik after losing everything on their homeworld of Gesú.

They need help finding a legacy or a choir to join, so that they can partake in the prayers offered to them. Lone gods have a hard time competing with hundreds working in tandem, though some manage.


Dream of Rain is, as their name suggests, a god of both dreams and rain, and here in this sleepless, weather-controlled land, they are spectacularly weak, but they've got nowhere else to go.
