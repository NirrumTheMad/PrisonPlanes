
The gulf trench race is one of the largest events of the year. It is powered by meat, machine, metaphor and magic. This year there is a new contestant. Both an-eagle themed woman, and of course, the party are involved in the events.

The party simply has to compete, people who were finites scarce months ago will cause a stir. Finites who win, or almost win, well, that's some good juice

The idea is that each aspect determines your race attacks and defences

Metaphor interacts with the track and its shape
Magic interacts with the obstacles
Machine interacts with the actual car and its mechanical issues
and meat navigates and survives. Your modifier to the dice roll is the  number of levels you have in each.
