
Conflict amulet: No one can know the secrets or allow others to build the framework to know the secrets

The Cult of the shining egg is a rather large parareligious organisation, which is impressive on a plane with so many gods. The cult organises around the custodial care and management of a large wall-born egg. The Egg seems to molt on occasion, shedding large and valuable chunks of thickly woven divinity. The chunks, the shell, can nourish gods and power intricate magics with little issue. Analysis of the Egg suggests that the being trying to hatch from it would predate upon the wall and probably *not* everything else around it. It is unprecedented either way and a secretive organisation has formed around it and seeks to not only keep it hidden, but protected, nourished, and uncapitalised upon (except for what the cult needs to sustain itself).


In order to do so, a lot of counterespionage work needs to be done
