### 🧱 FP Cost: 0

#### **Artisan’s Tools Proficiency**
- **Origin**: Artificer 1, Background  
- **Effect**: Gain proficiency with one set of artisan’s tools of your choice.  
- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Proficiency, Utility  
- **Notes**: Usually granted via class or background.

---

#### **Druidic**
- **Origin**: Druid 1  
- **Effect**: You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak and leave hidden messages in Druidic that only other druids can understand.  
- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Language, Thematic  
- **Notes**: Minimal mechanical impact.

---

#### **Musical Instrument Proficiency**
- **Origin**: Bard 1, Background  
- **Effect**: Gain proficiency with one musical instrument of your choice.  
- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Proficiency  
- **Notes**: Often cosmetic, can be thematic for certain spells.

---

#### **Tool Proficiency (Thieves’ Tools)**
- **Origin**: Rogue 1, Background  
- **Effect**: Gain proficiency with Thieves’ Tools.  
- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Proficiency, Utility  
- **Notes**: Essential for traps and locks.

---

#### **Tool Proficiency (Tinker’s Tools)**
- **Origin**: Artificer 1  
- **Effect**: Gain proficiency with Tinker’s Tools.  
- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Proficiency, Utility  
- **Notes**: Mostly thematic unless tied to specific features.

---

#### **Language: Thieves’ Cant**
- **Origin**: Rogue 1

- **Prerequisites**: None  
- **Tags**: Language, Thematic  
- **Notes**: Purely roleplay in most campaigns.

---

#### **Language Proficiency (Extra Language)**
- **Origin**: Background, Race, Favored Enemy (sometimes)

- **Notes**: Often determined by race/background.

---



#### **Unarmored Defense (Monk)**

---

#### **Ritual Casting (Prepared Caster)**
- **Origin**: Cleric, Druid

- **Origin**: Bard, Warlock

- **Tags**: Spellcasting, Utility  
- **Notes**: Separate from Prepared Caster ritual casting.
