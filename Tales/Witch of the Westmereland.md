You stumble in to a bit of a party event that seems entirely class agnostic, as many uncounted are milling about without a care. It is the Western office of the Great Coven's yearly Great Rock Banquet, held before the coven heads begin their yearly ritual on the lone mountain just outside of town. The event is ostensibly a potluck, but it is generally understood that two or three participants usually bring enough to stock the entire event on accident. 

At the event, some cool prizes are given out for costumes, but the event rather suddenly locks down when a hairpin of one of the local witch lords is gone missing, and spirits indicate that it has been stolen.

The event becomes a horrendous nightmare for everyone involved.

Goal: survive
Bonus goal: locate and apprehend the thief

The Thief is a rather skinny plasmid who has taken to hiding around some cabling underneath the floor of the main hall, which has become a bit of a nightmare hazard
