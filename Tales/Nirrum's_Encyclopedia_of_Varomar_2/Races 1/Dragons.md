# Dragons 
Created Tuesday 19 November 2024

Dragons on snarl are a diverse group of wildly different morphologies, all unified under a few phenomenotypical features.
	Dragons are mostly large, often larger than any homonid, though not all follow this trend.
	Dragons all have serpentine features, namely Scales, but these vary wildly
	Dragons all are somewhat territorial, but can often overcome this with complex social structures or by socialisation
	Dragons are all predatory, and apex predators within their size range (and often punch quite a bit above that)
	Dragons are all capable of some thaumatic manipulation, most often found in the form of flight and their breath weapon, 
	Draconic dialects are all mutually intelligible, despite being wildly different in sound.
	Draconic phenomenotypical influences usually come with several if not all of these features
	
Snarl's Dragon population changes signifincantly but some have been more normalised than others, here are some of the tribes, families, or types of dragons and some details about them


**Cloud Dragons**
	This is mostly a type of Dragon, as though they are quite intelligent and have quite a long historical record, they are the widest ranging of dragons, capable of staying aloft indefinitely and preferring to make their homes within clouds. They are hexapodal, having a large pair of wings as their First set of limbs (these replace silver dragons). They have a cultural feud with Stone Dragons (also translated as Mountain dragons) and will often go out of their way to fight them. 
	
	Cloud dragons are thickly furred, with fur growing along skin on the back of each scale. Their fur bears a variety of patterns, but usually they are silvery or grey to match the clouds they prefer to spend their time within. Cloud dragons are quite large, longer but thinner than Stone Dragons, and their ancient stages can eclipse towns. Cloud Dragons are often innate mages, and their connection with the sky has granted them a powerful breath weapon  that shoots a spray of supercooled moist air, often layering rime on contact. They have a long frill of spines along the back of their necks with sharp rear-facing jaw horns and a picklick chin horn

**River Lung**
	This is also mostly a type of dragon, Similarly intelligent to cloud dragons, they are wide ranging as well, but stick to oceans and waterways. They are quite capable of flight but tend to prefer swimming. They are also capable of transformation, disguising themselves in whatever form they wish. They are Quadrupeds with long bodies and large, thick scales, Frequently with antlers and long talons (replace Brass, Athinacaraman). They have long sensory whiskers and spectacularly sensitive eyes. 
	

**Minor Dragon Sp.**
	A small hexapodal dragon that is mostly insectivorous, but is well known for also taking on mice, snakes, and bats. Some species of minor dragon are capable of breathing fire.  They're not spectacularly intelligent and lack language but seem to be able to understand other dragons (who confirm their lack). Their scales vary between species, but the most common, the so-called teacup dragon has extremely small scales for its size. 
	
**Field Wyvern Sp.**
	Field dragons are medium sized Quadrupedal dragons, Usually green or brown in colour. They're intelligent enough have a language but mostly use it for announcing themselves to other dragons. Rather Uniquely, they are innately capable of learning other languages to fluency at an extremely rapid pace, outstripping even most telepathic beings, though they mostly *that* for demanding tribute of whatever kind they think they can get. Attempts to conscript them for library work is a story of failure told once every 200 years. They are capable of quick bursts of dragonfire, which they mostly use to intimidate and rid themselves of pests, which can get through their relatively thin scales
	
**Stone Dragons (replaces blue)**
	Stone dragons are a rather numerous species of dragon. They live in modestly large family groups with wide territories. Also called mountain dragons, stone dragons usually live in caves and spend long portions of their day shaping the stone to fit their needs. Stone dragons have narrow, relatively flexible scales piled thick in layers a bit like quills, Short but thick claws with gooves underneath, and brilliantly patterned wings. Two long Keratinous horns grow out of the back of the dragon's skull. These are prone to breaking and chipping but continuously grow. Stone dragons mostly use these horns for display, as well as to protect their necks and flanks.

**Numerous Dragon** 
	Refusing to be called Common, Numerous Dragons are Usually Mottled Green and Brown with a grand array of pigmentation patterns. They Are Hexapodal, and by far the most abundant clade of dragons on Snarl. They have a varied habitat, but are most at home in forests, particularly in rocky areas. They are capable of breath weapons, most frequently fire, but some seem to be dually capable of noxious gasses
