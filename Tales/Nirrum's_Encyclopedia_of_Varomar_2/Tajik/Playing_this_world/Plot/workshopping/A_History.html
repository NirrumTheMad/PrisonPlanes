A History

Created Tuesday 20 May 2025

A world Once Called Moðiod in one of its many languages found itself the victim of two sour fates. A passing star-corpse flung them and their system far into the universe long before they were born, and it had flung toward them into the maw of a waiting entity.

Magnum Raja. The Great King. Moðiod had dealt with such entities a few times before. Killing or imprisoning them. But the great king was wily, and impossibly powerful. Other titans seemed to be drawn to it, and in combat, it routinely bested them, and grew in power.

There was only a single thing that saved Moðiod from fading entirely. A single, hapless chronomancy student, studying time as a hobby had experimented upon himself. Inenzi Lodum, of Gundemit, woke from blinking to find half the sky black, the sun swollen, and his people and lands faded. MaRa's attention had long left this place. Slowly, by science and experience, he restored his world, and gained great fame. But MaRa returned, its belly unfilled, rehungered. Again, The world stopped, but this time, Inenzi the Sandy was prepared. Again he repaired his home, and again The Great King returned. It should have been trivial to defeat this titan the third time, but greed and endless suffering seemed to claw the world back into complacency. Inenzi thought to punishments that truly fit the crime that he had heard of, and at the time, relished learning of, and this time, when he went to revive the world, he had stipulations.

"You'll have to sign this paper and pay me to do this again, or I will leave you frozen until you are the last person to free. Your competitors will all have an advantage over you, if we get to you at all"
First he freed Lawyers and some like-minded mages and he set about building a world that would follow his instructions and commit to its own safety. A world that would do so out of greed and suffering, struggling for a moment of complacency. Make them do what they are good at, after all. With his unparalleled and increasing understanding of Magnum Raja's Power, he marched the entire world at knifepoint into an awful future, one of the few it could recover from.

The path to hierachical Monopoly was not Linear.

First there were still countries, Citystates, Conglomerates.

Hundreds of nations, Eight Major Oceans, Twelve significant Continents, Biodiverse habitats, Two massive shield volcanoes, Eishur and Èsukono, sites of immense reference and pilgrimage, later in their history known as the Eyes of the world.

Gods ended up being pretty frequent features in the corporate world as they are mostly sustained on Recognition. Occasionally they end up as co-workers with their opposition. In an attempt to Reduce hostility in the workplace in this increasingly common event, A sponsored get-together by Ergun Logistics and the Klesha Social network was held in Boubenana city, the longstanding capital of what was once Ežrimc. The result was catastrophic and many of the gods have never recovered and a few aren't done celebrating with their newfound friends, despite over seventy years clocked time having passed. Once the Machine Phenomenotypical wave density peaked rather close to the wall, and for nearly 200 years, nearly every forbidden creature was clearly engineered in strange ways. Several populations on Tajik are now partly or wholly mechanical.

The Nekolibrary was founded by a group of rebellious teens who watched an animated show from one of their parent's homeworlds about "Catgirls working in a library". It has become one of the most highly defended and rapidly dispersed resources on the planet. Parts of the library are written into viruses that will add themselves to other viruses, sometimes overwriting the original function of the virus. Since its inception it has become a game for youth, ignorant of the horrors of the corporate world, to find ways to keep the Nekolibrary alive. While the library is often targetted by subcompanies or individuals wishing to hide their mistakes and sins, its easy to distribute nature has made it exceptionally hard to get rid of any part of it. Inenzi, of course, funds and helps curate the library so that it doesn't get swamped with garbage information, Frequently turning other "criminal organisations" onto the scent of people trying to attack the library.

Inenzi's darkest hour was thinking that everything was for naught when the sun exploded, only to watch it freeze in place as a timestop event covered the world, bouncing off one of his many defenses. Work to restore the sun has been underway ever since, Additional mass and several Elech and arcanotech items are consistently being sourced and engineered from corporations in pursuit of this goal.

Inenzi found immortality rather early, but most of his original party are dead. One of them, the time sorcerer is simply adrift.

Asurenda Arms was originally founded by an Asurenda , who was horrifically aware of how dire the situation was that the best thing she could do was make weapons. While no member of the board since has shared his understanding and insight, all of the CEOs have had more than four limbs, Usually by quite a bit.

Rather late in the timeline, The army of the small finally made enough of a dent in the infrastructure of Hemma, Cracking open a massive market for low-sand, low-volume workers who could build condos in most kitchens. Travel times for the small are nightmarish, so most work from home. Most of the small are less than 30cm tall, as that is about where nearly all infrastructure becomes entirely useless to them. It is not infrequent for the bottom drawer of a cabinet to be rented out in an apartment

A recurring problem is the 8 Winged Sentinel, an apocalypse-class entity, made of compound eyes on veinous stalks, connected to large "feathered" wings (which are actually modified scales). It seems remarkably able to recover from stolen time events, as Inenzi has fought it personally six times, and now made a task force to deal with it.

During the Blossoming mountain war, Named for one of the spells cast which last hundreds of clocked years afterward, The value of Graviturges was exemplified, when both sides came together to defeat a massive storm of world-buster meteors. As the war raged on, many of these mages went missing, only to turn up in Tajik's employ.

Halfway through the clocked history of the world as Tajik, The Wall erupted in massive hooked tentacles, threatening to engulf the world, the star, and several worlds around. This is the only time in known history that Inenzi has talked Magnum Raja Down from attacking, and this preceded a brief truce. The wall's newest emission was dealt with, and a week without sanding or incursions followed.

Brefanario is said to have hid a repository of the native species on Tajik somewhere, either in speciment or in DNA, but it is unlikely they have been sanded at any point, and quite possible to have eroded under the weight of stolen time. Truthfully, the library is fully intact, thanks to a clever use of geothermal energy.

The Revolt of the Tempters was a fairly minor war not long after Tajik's Current spatial arrangement was set. As things from across many worlds came to the edge of all things that can be known as we know them, the power of the world grew, and soon enough "can cut mountains" was a "good start" for a swordsman. Soon, the powers of Great devils weren't capable of recreating what ambitious people needed. In an attempt to keep their magic relevant, these Tempters set about sabotaging much of the technology in the world. EcastE was the corporation that was able to track them down, securing a staggering amount of "insurance points" which translated to a lot of sand, before the system was fully understood by most corporations, which set them on their course to Monopoly. Many of those tempters are still at large but ineffective against anyone above uncounted.

The great coven is the largest Mages Co-operative on the planet. Gaining official recognition about 2/3rds of the way to modern time, They lack the formality of a Wizard's university because they operate on more holistic research principles. The Great Coven operates in a singular sphere. The Collection of holistic practicioners and practices of Magic, offering resources to those that do.

The Tajika's influence on the world's politics was not revealed until the Sea-war, When nearly a hundred coastal nations found themselves increasingly inalnd and suddenly without the availability of their most useful trading and food resource as the Mejja flood overtook the other half of the world, the side without stars. When many of the nations involved in chasing the shoreline had their chiefs quietly invited to a massive banquet hosted by the Tajika, the Group was almost killed on the spot, save for the timely intervention of one of the Tajika's Allies, the Golden Luckless Dragon, Yazi.

Several Independent Phenomenotypical Influence Flux Events (PIFE) Barrage the plane. "Cephalopod" is one of the only recognisable types for most people. Brand new types of "psionic" abilities emerge.

The Turtle Deva arrives on the planet, walking in through a hell attached to a world that no longer exists, carrying every spirit that remained after that world's own encounter with the wall. All Twelve of them. One of these Twelve would found the Targarm media group, later, Tagarm Entertainment. the others were largely lost to history.

In a deal with Sword, the Turtle Deva agrees to house the military fortress, The Answer of Earth to Heaven, named from the Turtle's own wishes.

The Romance of the Steelborn Knight and Golden God-One Shehalami becomes one of the most legendary tragedies in the planet's history. Steelborn Knight was an automaton starfish, with an extremely unorthodox combat style. It came from a world where legends described a 14 limbed, gold-ringed god, imagined differently by the people who first wrote of it. Upon Meeting the 12-handed, two legged Shehalami, the baggage of a dead world had to reconcile with a living legend. Across 150 years, the steelborn knight served Shehalami who knew of the knight's feelings. A true and honest care from the starfish was more than enough to sustain Shehalami, who appreciated the confidant, unbothered and unhindered by things like insurance class. But traditions across the stars are fickle, and when steelborn knight was wounded in battle, he asked Shehalami to eat him, as was custom. In his people's world, this meant to be absorbed into another intelligence. This was unknown, untranslatable, and when Shehalami obliged, she was able to feel the despair and remorse from the misunderstanding in the last moments of her prized friend's life. She mourned as her own people did, through suicidal war. For a hundred more years, Shehalami destroyed untold enemies and impossible odds, against those considered friend and foe, anyone who could grant her death. In the end it was a descendant of Halet who was able to subdue her, and Inenzi himself who calmed her down and walked her through her grief. Stars-and-Gold has been an idiom for unbearable grief, and has made its way into song and story ever since.

The internet was founded by a spirit who ordered a TV and several Connectors to a curious child's house, after living in their phone for two years. When the child was interviewed by police about this machine, they constructed their own, and the spirit was able to communicate with both. Many longstanding internet protocols take this spirit's comfort into account.

It has taken hundreds of attempts to get right. The tea is good enough to disrupt their battle, and will only get better in the next forty attempts.
