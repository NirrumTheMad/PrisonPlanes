Gutterman is, obviously, a bhodisattva who lives off of begging. He is fully aware of the campaign's endings. Gutterman knows how to look for the gaps that people can't normally see, and is vaguely aware of the power structure the party has landed in. With this in mind he is able to give very limited insight on answers of what one *should do* in order to achieve the super secret endings.

He will only tell people why things are morally the right thing to do, following the rules of Karma, and he'll advise people about how to spot traps. 

"If they want this... what do they want it for? Is that what they told you? Why did they want to tell you that?"

"Ahhh, killing. Always an option, never a good one, never did me any good, and I've been here for a long time."

He has no enemies, will never be targetted, and rather conspicuously to people who level up high in the *metaphor* path, is never worth involving in plots.
