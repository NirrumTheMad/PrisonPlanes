
The Iudex, The judge, is a community centre and gym chain that has been built by many deities of both prosocial and antisocial bent. Originally built from the skeleton of a very large hell, the souls once inside have been replaced with more profitable gym memberships (cheap, none the less) for people who *want* to struggle with physical tasks, some of immense difficulty and potential danger. Even More befuddling to the original Devilish Deity that built the place was when Multiple similar hells and a few heavens wanted to combine their efforts. Those who value struggle and those value strength can indeed find common ground. Do not fall in the Pit outside. It is the stairs for giants. Bathrooms are available for 63 recognised common morphological brackets. 

* Pools - Waterlogged hells of varying temperatures, stretching beyond the reach of eyes. Some of them have predators or grasping hands for additional challenges. Some of them have a shallow end. 
* Weights - Descending in floors to decrease the cost of the infrastructure, these vary from weights more suited to [[The Small]] to Stars-on-Bars. There are resistive machines that can power the entire facility 
* Courts, Rings, Sports - The courts are the most expensive part of the facilities at any given Iude. Nearly a hundred in each location, each extremely durable and configurable to hundreds of thousands of different sports. Sports equipment is kept extremely well maintained (both as judicial punishment and as a job) in hidden mechanised panels within the walls. The catch is that it is a very hefty sum to have a fully private match, where a pantheon of creatures here to watch you struggle is *not* present. This costs more depending on the type of sport, with fights of any kind having an additional tier simply to keep the audience quiet.
* Tracks - There are many forms of equipment for running, treadmills, wheels, etc; but one of the most popular are the infinite tracks. Made of empty afterlives, these extradimensional spaces were purchased cheap. Some still have less intelligent harrier demons within, for those who want added challenges or conditions. Some of these resemble sprawling forests, a rare trace of autotrophy in this bureauphagic world, some vast deserts, some as frigid mountains. More than a few are just gym classes filled with screaming child-shaped demons.
* Climbing, Cages, Flight Runs - Less attention was paid to these. But within the tracks, Often, hanging cages, chains, and hooks, once replete with tormented beings, are now simply hanging. Walls made to be impossible to climb are, to some species, simply difficult, and bottomless pits of steel rungs have been the source of more than one person going missing for a few months before returning absolutely jacked, though on occasion, there are deaths.
