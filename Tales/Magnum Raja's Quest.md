[[Tajik/Playing_this_world/Magnum_Raja|Magnum_Raja]] is, technically almost the entire universe, or at least, this aspect of Magnum Raja is capable of understanding a projection of the universe that it was once almost fully agent over. Magnum Raja's existence might imply the existence of a universe outside of this one through random event 69, but it should be thought of as merely the transference of a dream to a dream within a dream within a dream. The passing of a dream to a different dreamer. 

Magnum Raja is an old identity that has lost much of what it used to be to preserve what it is. It is alive, Barely. All beings, all places, all events, are divergent fragments of itself, and once it stops their fleeing, it can rest, and then return to its previous life, which it barely remembers. Its name, probably unpronounceable. Its form, unrecognizable. 

Magnum Raja wants to undo all plans, freeze all events, and regain the universe that was once its own flesh as its flesh again. It has run into one continuously infuriating enemy, [[Nirrum's_Encyclopedia_of_Varomar_2/Tajik/Playing_this_world/Plot/workshopping/A_History/Inenzis_Quest|Inenzi]], the most prolific and tired of the Tajika, who once wounded it so badly that it lost nearly everything it had regained, and was stuck to this fragile core on this single, awful world with its back to [[The Wall]]. 

To do so, it must first Break Inenzi's interference, [[The Tajik Corporation]], the Legacy of the Tajika, which the world carried to the edge of the universe, only to smash MaRa against it's wall. Time Wasters.  

Lofty goals, such as the capture of Inenzi, the corruption of the remaining settlements, or even the destabilisation of the green ring have proven dangerous an infeasible. Providing friction to Inenzi's deployment of resanding technologies has been far more effective. Dodging the Chyman curves built around it have proven beyond its ability, despite otherworldly and alien intelligence. Persistence, Attrition, and Occasional innovations in the methods by which it can kill and/or freeze creatures have proven the most effective. 

Its plan to concentrate and attenuate the sand and dust that persistently  restart its hard-won wars is a perpetual chore, but one it has thus far made ground on.
