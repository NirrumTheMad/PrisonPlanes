"Paüli, Get up, we've got a job."
Immediately, I miss the dream I was having. Gōska's voice is immensely grating. Before I can even fully gain consciousness, I've got boots, pants, body armor, and a coat on. It takes me a minute to take off the body armor and put a shirt. Then slide the armour back on. I ask what the job is. Gōska seems surprised I asked but answers, "Knife Crate Delivery"

There is nothing more boring a job than a knife crate delivery. Every single person must have a sealing knife on them at all times. The problem is that Turnover here is high. Knives get lost all the time. New people show up all the time. Knives get made all the time. Shipped all the time. Every business has to have a small crate of them near the door at all times. It doesn't matter who delivers them, so every Chōba takes the job when it comes up. It's enough sand for a few days, and you *always* get paid. *Always*. It does matter if a nova flare flash-boils every single person who you saw that day, that money *will* be in your account. I guess it's one of the few things more reliable than death here. I hear that that's an improvement over most planets. 

It feels like I blinked. We're at the depot, I can taste ScunGees EconoGees in my teeth. I don't remember eating. It's just a thing I do now. The lineup is already long. It's not often I come to the centre of town for anything. The Tajik loading lot is pretty much the only place I have a reason to be in here. I don't have reason to be here for long. Long lines of cable lash out from the bay, anchoring at predetermined points. My dad once told me that the Tajik folks learned cabling from an ancient hero over near Chich. I once dreamt of being able to do that. I guess I still do sometimes. The gondolas streak out of the bay with ferocious speed, navigating the precisely placed, perfectly timed web without a single flaw or hitch. They say nothing ever brakes in the Tajik office. A loud thunk shakes the Gendra. My hands are already moving, Straps hold down the crate, Locks on the bed are engaging the standardized holes. One gets stuck and can't deploy. I hope the straps hold. It doesn't matter, Gōska's already driving. I hope my boots will hold me down. I slip inside the cab and buckle in. 

A notification slips into my filters directing us to a spot back near the edge of town. Damn, probably not going to get more than one run. Unlucky. Gōska hits the accel and we shoot down the street as fast as the Gendra can go, admittedly, not very fast. A Doëmeg 2550 blasts past us. Our Clock slightly desyncs and our radio warps, the music lowering in pitch as it slows, then speeds up. Someone's going somewhere in a hurry, probably a less important one. I'd love to know what sort of job one needs to have to get one of those, or really, anything better than a Groller. 

"Ey," Gōska says, hitting me with his elbow, "think we uh... need to speed it up?"

I give him a flat look. There is nowhere for the accelerator to go. No room for any sort of rune or enchantment to slot in, no extra sw-.... there is an extra switch on the dashboard. It looks like it was glued in last night. Gōska reaches for it with a large shit eating grin on his face. I now know why the lock didn't engage

I wasn't out long, I couldn't've been, because we hadn't  turned off of main street, but we were a full quarter of the way there.
