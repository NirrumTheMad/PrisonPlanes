

The internet connections around the city are operated by whatever company owns the buildings in the area. 

Micro-sand charges are made toward the owners and operators of the target server, so most functions tend to host local internets and then use private bridges to wider cyberspace


It is expected that people who are running online spaces are collecting your data as you use them, and it is generally rude (or bannable) to try to circumvent this. It is also expected that you try to circumvent this to some degree. This back and forth interplay really just means that everyone needs to keep their systems up-to-date by buying the newest ECastE, 

If no one is watching a wire, it's free to use. It might be a trap, but for a staggering number of companies and places, there are unmonitored access points that get lost in the daily processing or cost too much to recover. 

Internet power usage is generally extremely lightweight per message, as there are so many passive obfuscation methods at once, it is very difficult to trace any individual activity the less local it is. This leads to the sheer irony that it is easier to be unnoticed on the internet than it is in meatspace
