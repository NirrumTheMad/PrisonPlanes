The Tajik Corporation, for which the world now known as Tajik draws its name, is the most powerful corporation across a great many worlds. It offers one major service and several minor ones. The first and foremost is an insurance policy that decides the order by which people get recovered from Stolen Time Events and the management of those efforts. The minor services are potent goods that assist in that goal. Namely Lithosaad staves, Sealing knives, Robes and Technologies for quick resanding.
