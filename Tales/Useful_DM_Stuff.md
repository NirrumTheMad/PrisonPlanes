"Each try is <TIMESPAN>"

Like a lockpick, Each try is 20 minutes. Rather, that's how long you can try before you guaranteedly fail, and how long it takes in between tries. This gives characters a reason not to try every six seconds. Rather, one try becomes 20 minutes, and sometime in that 20 minutes, depending on how successful you are, you either succeed or fail.
