3. Chyman's Algorithm. A powerful emergent and everchanging calculation that challenges the notion of incompetence and malice. Chyman's algorithm quantifies whether or not it is more likely that a mistake is a mistake, malice, or someone else's plan. It has had untold casualities and false positives, but it has increasingly few false negatives. Knowing another company's adjustments to Chyman's algorithm means knowing exactly where that company's blindspots are. The Chyman algorithm outputs a 4D Graph, time x CBA x Echo-shift x Downtime, where time shows the length of time the algorithm has been evaluating, CBA is a number from 0-1 that shows the result of a complex cost benefit analysis, Echo-shift shows a value of energy that approximates harvestable potential if someone makes a move, and downtime shows the amount of time that the company cannot track the individual.
