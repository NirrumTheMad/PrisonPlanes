Clinging to this mortal realm, Rysvyne, Alternatively Rysevyne or Risevine, is one of the greatest champions of the restless dead, and has levelled in a new sphere, Myth. Much like [[Salaban]] who he is immortally in opposition against, Rysvyne builds his power out of the remaining legacies of the dead, though unlike salaban, Rysevyne does not simply *take* power.
Rysevyne *coerces, lures, and beckons* power into his the seemingly bottomless folds of his misty cloak.

Conflict amulet:
Rysvyne is an antagonist and seeks to disturb and raise all those resting dead to spread through the universe as a plague, seeking simply to sustain itself at the cost of all else.

Rysvyne's quest currently targets Hemma's entertainment complexes, seeking to increase the dissatisfaction the living have, to rob them of inspiration. This means targetting Enser communications and Tagarm entertainment, Starting with Tagarm

To defeat Tagarm without boosting Enser, Rysvyne must:
	Devalue the investment
	Target the infrastructure
	Confound and Burden the agents of celebrities
	Encourage the illicit trade of copyright thieves and slopAI
	Target datacenters to reduce viability of streams
