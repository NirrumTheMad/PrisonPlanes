====== Legacies ======
Created Wednesday 28 May 2025

Billions of gods sit harvesting the ambient energy of their pantheon, weaving it from the universal wind as it hits the wall. There are Hundreds of Thousands of Legacies, which describe the path the powers take.
Here are some notable ones


==== //Balance of Rivers. Collected Torrent. Split Currents Rush to fill. // ====
		The song of gods who never die, but sleep endlessly, wake for centuries. Endlessly remade, the Balance of Rivers Legacy carries common themes of River Gods, life sustainers who allow delicate balances around inevitable forces of nature. A river never dies, it is simply the collection of water as it drips down to gravity, and so long as water is water and gravity is gravity, and a drop of heat exists, there will //always// be a river, slowly moving that water around. The legacy roughly collects pantheons of this nature:
			* A river changes everything around it, Know your place and others will figure out what that means for them
			* Take in all that comes, no matter what that means to what lies downstream. The river changes but it will survive.
			* Serenity, Turbulence, both words are meaningless, as they chase each other. Unambiguously, The River does as the River will
			* A convergence of rivers is just a larger river
			* A river never leaves a hole within itself, it is never empty.

==== Birth of Prey. Concentration of Energy Becomes Complex Systems. Care Begets Compassion ====
	Agriculture, Farm, Food, Hunting. Gods of sustenance have crafted this legacy. Many carry aspects of solar light, but this is a more meager aspect of what they stand for. All beings need to channel energy to live. Storing energy for hard times is a common trait. Where simple light becomes stored food for autotrophs, their predators will harvest it for their own, and their predators will harvest them. When the predator protects the prey, promises it recompense for its sacrifice, The path away from suffering for all creatures opens. 
		* Take what can be grown and grow it, change it to provide
		* Provide what can be given to promote growth
		* Build yourself so you may build others.
		* Conservation of the origin of resources is key
		* Take from the immortal to promote the mortal. 

==== End of all. Certainty from truth promotes action. Monolithic inheritance ====
	Death, the transition from one form to another, the ensured impermanence of all things. The Grave has long been the source of many gods, but this legacy is one of action, not submission. You //will// die. Whatever essence you think of as yourself will change its form into something that is not what you intended. The same is true of all things. Know their path to the grave, and you will know what action they will take.
		* All moments are your last. Act accordingly
		* All inheritences will fade, some will last longer than others
		* All forms that exist are exciting in their impermanence
		* Something will always be, but rarely will it be anything you recognise.
		* Defiance of the grave is the privilege of life. Use it before you must hand it on to whoever reaches for it after you.
		* Fear of anything else is unbecoming.
