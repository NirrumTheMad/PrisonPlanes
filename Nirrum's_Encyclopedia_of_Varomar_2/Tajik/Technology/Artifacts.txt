**Vajrakhanda**
	Artifact, Melee Weapon
		The Perfect, unlimited sword. Its swordliness would take stars worth of energy to surpass, it is that close to the phenomenotypical sword. Simply by holding it, one becomes a part of it. You do not weild it so much as perform it, physical though it is. All of the Asurenda arms Vajra line are imitations that do not compare.
