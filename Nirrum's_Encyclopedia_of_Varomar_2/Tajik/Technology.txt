The Tajik Company, in the world of Tajik, technology is king, and Tajik has long been the king of Technology.
In their perpetual desire to fight back the Demon of the End of Time, Magnum Raja, the Tajik company encouraged, fostered and hunted the advancement of technologies that could help defeat it. This blend of every form of power manipulation, From Spreadsheet to Prayer, has given them a special relationship with all things around them.


==== Internet. ====
	The internet is ubiquitous beyond all sense, such that the knowledge of these people is almost always available. Interconnectivity is omnipresent, save in rare cases.
