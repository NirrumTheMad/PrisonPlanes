Vehicle classes for different uses
	Econ
		1 has the ability to go forward
		2 has the ability to go forward *and* a roof* 
	Econ fancy
		1 the ability to go forward and it *glows*
		2 has the ability to go forward and a roof and maybe even a spoiler!
	Better
		The Most standard car that exists
	Better Fancy
		The Most standard car that'll last a little longer than the most standard car
	Sport
		1 Turns weird but it's good if you can get used to it, goes fast
		2 Never turns weird ever, decently fast 
		3 Impossible to flip
	Sport fancy
		1 This goes so fast but turning is more of an art than a science
		2  Accelerates so smoothly it is hard to tell that you're going fast
	Ultrasport fancy
		1 Fastest, never flip
		2 So very fast, cannot feel a bump lower than half the tire height 
	Utility small econ
		Has a trailer
	Utility Small
		Has a pan
	Utility med
		can hold a large amount of things
	Utility Seater
		can hold more people than average
	Utility large
		Probably could be lived in
	Utility Large Seater
		Frequently gets lived in
	Utility massive
		Not Legal on some roads, holds much
	Utility Ultramassive
		Not legal on most roads, which are a suggestion to it anyway
	Weird
		Scrambler - nondestructive climber. Not very fast, but faster in dense neighbourhoods
		Hrover - hovercar, drift is the only option, not legal on city streets
		Tam Lin - Hoverbike, the fastest thing to move in a straight line
		The Flea - Jumps high, fast, and far. Can only turn when landed *sucks to ride*
