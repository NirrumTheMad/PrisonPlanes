====== Fuwa ======
Created Friday 23 May 2025

One of the Smaller Continents consisting of a single tall mountain range in the west and a hilly slope moving to the east. Imagine China rotated and tilted into the sea. 

Now, the peaks of Fuwa are eroded by the light of the Mortal Star which shines directly on it.
