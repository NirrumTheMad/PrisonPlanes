====== Rincewind Questline ======
Created Sunday 13 April 2025

This obvious discworld reference has the formula that Rincewind is always attacked by forbidden creatures, understands all languages, is incapable of magic or death.

Rincewind needs to get to a hospital to treat his condition which he is a bit embarassed to talk about.  A golaar spawns on the way.

Rincewind sends a //handwritten letter which is stained where he obviously rubbed road dirt into it in an attempt to pay, which has evidently worked//. He needs to get a cellphone, locate some ants in a sewer, two flowers, and an insurance plan, a GLOBBY HORROR spawns on the way

Rincewind calls. His voice is very distant as he is holding the phone upside down.  A hoard of Sheehom has descended upon the library he has found work at.

Rincewind calls again, he thinks he's found a partial cure for for his condition but it involves retrieving an artefact from a strangely intact tomb.
