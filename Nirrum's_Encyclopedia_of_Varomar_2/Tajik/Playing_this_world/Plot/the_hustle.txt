====== the hustle ======
Created Saturday 29 March 2025

	After collecting character sheets and examining backstories, the plot follows from getting them together.
	Ideally they become roommates. To facilitate this, show a list of apartments at a rate of some sand per month. One of them, rather importantly, boasts a furnished kitchen, working sewage with two water closets, heated water, and laundry facilities nearby. The Deal is too good to be true... because it's above a funerary factory, processing (usually) bodies for resources day and night. The Smell is truly awful. Despite the smell, the obviously converted office space has a balcony overlooking a wide shipping yard. A single Devil's Paintbrush weed grows from accumulated grime in one corner. It also has a window directly above the industrial dumpster seven floors below, on another street. It also boasts a roof access, though this is mostly occupied by hvac and two Massive smokestacks, though there is some space.
