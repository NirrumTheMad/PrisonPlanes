[[+A_History]] 
Mattress/bedding upgrades allow you to recover more hit die per day
Food upgrades for certain resources?

YOU CAN ONLY LEVEL UP IF YOU GET AN UPGRADED 
	* bed - resting place
	* computer - analysis system
	* At least thirty meals
	* Clothing - covering, armor, fashion
	* Implement - sword, staff, claws. 
Players can trigger one random event a night at will, One random event will happen per night guaranteed.
Inspiration points can also be spent to trigger them

All permanent deaths become part of the final boss. 
Alternately, the parts of the party that escape each death are part of the final boss

All creatures the party Kills (other than the forces of MaRa) add to the strength of the final boss

The internet is controlled by whatever company owns the buildings in the area. Any Internet service provider that has ever tried to crop up has been //rapidly torn to shreds. //
