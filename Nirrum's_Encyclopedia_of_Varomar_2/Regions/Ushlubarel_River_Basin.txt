====== Ushlubarel River Basin ======
Created Saturday 19 October 2024

The Valley of the aegis mountains. Where orcus landed with a world-shattering step. Here, three great curses were laid here, issued from the great maw of the foul demon. The curses were on the land, on the gods that came here, and the last one was on orcus himself when he realized what it was he was likely to unleash. Anathema to his quiet universe. Teruna, the Demon that keeps things alive and screaming.

The Ushlubarel river basin varies mostly from north to south, central to perimeter. 


===== Ecology =====
	The valley slopes are craggy but well forested, rich and fertile, but hostile, and fairly resistant to farming. While the topsoil is rich, and the weather fairly consistent, the rainfall is often not quite enough to sustain agriculture year over year without careful planning. In the spring, when rain is the thickest, great migrations trigger, for some notable species
		* Giant Elk - These large moose-like cervids rush to get the new sprouts of spring, running to the south where some may have recovered from the winter earlier. As the summer progresses they wander far in small herds, only to rush north in the autumn to collect the fruits that begin to fall throughout the southern portion of the region in that time,
		* Black River Crab - Giant migratory land crabs that Move south during the winter, feeding on detritus and whatever they can reasonably get their claws on. Black River crabs are slow, and contrary to their name, quite at home away from rivers, so long as there is something damp they can wet their mouths with. Trees have developed rather thick bark to protect against the crushing claws, and houses are built such that they are difficult to grab, not that any settlement worth their salt didn't invent crab picks within the first year of the crabs eating any thatching or any animal that couldn't get out of its reach in time. Black River Crabs can get up to three meters wide at their largest, but rarely do they surpass two meters. During the earliest steps of winter, they can be found covered in piles of broken branches as they molt. During this time they are covered in an insulating layer of fat and tough skin, mostly to prevent their own protective building from puncturing them. 
		* Warrior Crab - A second set of Giant migratory land crabs that move //north// during the late summer. Capable of jumping four to five times their body length from still, these crabs are ambush predators as well as scavengers. They tend to stick to rivers and that makes them a favourite food of the nomadic Orc Cultures of the Ushlubarel valley, which follow the crab and elk migrations. They hunt mostly fish, water birds, and animals that get close to the rivers, particularly near rocky gorges. They cannot run for very long nor are they particularly fast runners, so in pursuit they restort to jumping, and only a few times. Warrior crabs can grow up to a meter and a half wide, with many reaching this within the first two years of their lives. These crabs hibernate in the northern rivercourse close to the Uolakomara range, and sometimes find themselves falling into crevices into the various cave systems
		* Uslubarel Salmon - Largely blind andadromous salmonids that rush the ushlubarel every spring. Unlike many salmonids, these don't die after their transformation from the ocean, and can live for decades. Rarely, an //emperor// salmon gets spotted, often thrashing boats smaller than it is. These salmon are ones that have reached over thirty years of age, around which time they undergo a Transformation into aggressive shepherds of the yearly spawning run, gaining massively in size, vision, and even a small amount of intelligence as they begin to operate more tactically. While they are still capable of laying eggs of their own, they can only do so in the deeper and wider parts of the ushlubarel rather than in the proper rocky streambeds that feed into it. Still, the transformation benefits the species. Emperor Ushlubarel salmon are a major threat to any small vessel or low flying creature near the ushlubarel. They are capable of leaving the water for minutes at a time and can reach human running speeds for up to a minute, but usually wriggle their way back to the water as soon as they've scared whatever they were targetting off. Often they do this to harangue crabs, deer, and even sapient species if they're even slightly close to the water and within their limited vision. Normally only a few of these get spotted each season, and most ushlubarel salmon of spawning age range from a skyling at 30cm, to a Kors, at a meter long. Particularly fat Kors are nearing the end of their lifespan as a normal Ushlubarel salmon as they store up resources to transform into their emperor stage.
		* Dwarf Heron - Human-sized herons that feed on the salmon. They bear beautiful purple striped crests and Brown-white-Orange striped wings that they use in their courtship display. The Lelumadil take great pride in capturing and taming these creatures, but only the rich can afford to feed them fish, so most often they are let go within a year, only to visit for a few days during their migration. Enough of them make this journey that other civilizations have noted it, hence their name.These nest deep in ganglegrove each winter
		* Razalast's tit - this small songbird is largely insectivorous and prefers the high treelines along the aegis' slopes. It has a distinctive orange stripe down its back and onto its tail. Strangely, this stripe glows blue in the pesence of orcs, half orcs, and silk. It flies south during the winters
		* Leomon's wandering mink - an exceptionally long mustelid with an extra pair of legs in the middle of its body. It's quite adapted to both land and water and has an exceptionally tough hide, giving it resistance to piercing and slashing damage. This one follows the warrior crab migration for calcium from crabs that die
		* Inland Valley Roc - Larger in wingspan than the common or desert roc, the Inland Valley Roc is a native of the Ushlubarel, and has evolved a significantly longer beak and longer legs than the Common Roc for use in the forests. It feeds almost exclusively on Crabs. During the winter its metabolism slows and it can often be found walking along the southern Aegis foothills poking through piles of treefall to find molting black river crabs. During the summer it likes to fly much more and can be found all over the valley. If it spots a crab it wants, it'll maneuver it into its claws, then  soar exceptionally high into the sky, only to drop the crab somewhere it can reach the smashed carcass easily. Trees in the ushlubarel have all evolved to survive such crashes, as well as the winds that come from the Roc's flight. 
	Non-Migratory animals also live in the Ushlubarel basin, and while their lives are shaped by the migratory animals, these ones prefer to keep to  their own territories
		* Lop Deer - Lop deer are strange in that they shed and regrow their antlers quite frequently, with the exception of a forward-curving spike which they use in self defense against crabs that attack their herds. These forward curving spikes do not have the same alchemical properties as a unicorn, which they can be mistaken for. They graze on tree tips all through the ushlubarel all year long, and frequently gnaw on crab molts. 
		* Riverbear - A reddish furred bear that is exceptional at swimming, and frighteningly fast. It's smaller than many other bear species, even some in the area, but it is fierce when it is attacking fish. It seems to have evolved the ability to teleport short distances, not more than its own body length, usually using this to escape larger crabs
		* Phosphor Hawk - This hawk has very strange territorial habits. It does not like other hawks in its territory, but it that territory must always overlap with another Phosphor hawk's. If a hawk finds another phosphor hawk in its territory, it'll send a warning shriek, which if ignored becomes an aerial fight. Normally though, these shrieks are heeded, with the intruder returning to its territory. If a Phosphor hawk spots another Phosphor hawk being attacked, it will relentlessly defend the waylaid hawk. Phosphor hawks release small gouts of flaming oil from beneath their wings which spread wide and then flash-burn. The light is blinding, the fire is hot, and their talons are quite strong. Once the attacked hawk is freed, intruders will continually check up on and even feed and groom the injured hawk until they're strong and capable enough to chase them off again. On very rare occasions, multiple phosphor hawks can descend on a single attacker and start forest fires with the excessive oil, though these tend to not travel too far. 
		* Blackwater Dolphin - This river Dolphin's eyes have converged on the top of its head. While these only live in the main channels of the ushlubarel basin, they are still something to watch out for. Violent, Voracious, slightly playful, quite capable of barter and their own language, these dolphins hunt the smaller fish in the river, using echolocation and of course, small thunder spells which can be heard throughout the entire river as a series of pops.
